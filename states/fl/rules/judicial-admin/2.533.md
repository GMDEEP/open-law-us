# Rule 2.533 Oaths And Affirmations In Court

Oaths and affirmations to jurors and witnesses in court may be administered by or before:

(a) any judge, clerk, or deputy clerk of any court within this state; or (b) any person authorized to administer oaths in the State of Florida.
