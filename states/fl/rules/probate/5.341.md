# Rule 5.341 Estate Information

On reasonable request in writing, the personal representative shall provide an interested person with information about the estate and its administration.

Committee Notes This rule is not intended to overrule the holdings in In re Estate of Shaw, 340 So. 2d 491 (Fla. 3d DCA 1976), and In re Estate of Posner, 492 So. 2d 1093 (Fla. 3d DCA 1986). Rule History 2002 Revision: New rule.
