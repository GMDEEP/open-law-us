# Rule 5.330 Execution By Personal Representative

Notwithstanding any other provisions of these rules, the personal representative must sign the:

(a) inventory;

(b) accountings;

(c) petition for sale or confirmation of sale or encumbrance of real or personal property;

(d) petition to continue business of decedent;

(e) petition to compromise or settle claim;

(f) petition to purchase on credit;

(g) petition for discharge; and (h) resignation of personal representative.
