# Rule 8.705 Commencement Of Proceedings

(a) Petition to Be Filed. All proceedings under this part shall be initiated by the filing of a petition for the appointment of a guardian advocate.

(b) File to Be Opened. Upon commencement of any proceeding, the clerk shall open a file and assign a case number.
