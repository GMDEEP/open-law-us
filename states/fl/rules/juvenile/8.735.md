# Rule 8.735 Review And Removal

(a) Review by Court. The court may review the appointment of a guardian advocate at any time but shall review the appointment within the time limits as provided by law.

(b) Reauthorization or Removal. The reauthorization or removal of the guardian advocate shall be governed as provided by law. B. JUDICIAL WAIVER OF PARENTAL NOTICE OF TERMINATION OF PREGNANCY
