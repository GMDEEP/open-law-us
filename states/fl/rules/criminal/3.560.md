# Rule 3.560 Discharge Of Jurors

After the jurors have retired to consider their verdict, the court shall discharge them from the cause when:

(a) their verdict has been received;

(b) on the expiration of such time as the court deems proper, if the court finds there is no reasonable probability that the jurors can agree on a verdict; or (c) a necessity exists for their discharge. The court may in any event discharge the jurors from the cause if the prosecuting attorney and the defendant consent to the discharge.

Committee Notes 1968 Adoption. Same as section 919.21, Florida Statutes, except (4) omitted. 1972 Amendment. Same as prior rule.
