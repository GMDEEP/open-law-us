# Rule 3.391 Selection Of Foreperson Of Jury

The court shall instruct the jurors to select one of their number foreperson.

Committee Notes 1968 Adoption. This rule was inserted in order to clarify the system of selecting jury foreman. 1972 Amendment. Same as former rule 3.390.
