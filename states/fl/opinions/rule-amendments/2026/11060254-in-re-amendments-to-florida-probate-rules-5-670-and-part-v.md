# In Re: Amendments to Florida Probate Rules 5.670 and Part V

Supreme Court of Florida
                             ____________

                          No. SC2026-0065
                            ____________

  IN RE: AMENDMENTS TO FLORIDA PROBATE RULES 5.670
                     AND PART V.

                          November 12, 2026

PER CURIAM.

     The Florida Bar’s Probate Rules Committee proposes

amending Florida Probate Rule 5.670 (Termination of Guardianship

on Change of Domicile of Resident Ward) and Part V (Forms). 1 Both

the Committee and the Court published the proposal for comments

and none were received.

     We amend the rules as proposed by the Committee. Rule

5.670(d) is amended to add a statutory reference to section

744.524, Florida Statutes (Termination of guardianship on change

of domicile of resident ward). Part V is amended to replace the

phrase “without prolixity” to “concisely” for ease of understanding

      1. We have jurisdiction. Art. V, § 2(a), Fla. Const.; see also
Fla. R. Gen. Prac. & Jud. Admin. 2.140(b).

and to reference Rule Regulating The Florida Bar 10-2.2 (Form

Completion by a Nonlawyer) for instances where the forms are

completed by nonlawyers.

     Accordingly, we amend the Florida Probate Rules as reflected

in the appendix to this opinion. New language is underscored;

deleted language is stricken through. The amendments shall

become effective on January 1, 2027, at 12:01 a.m.

     It is so ordered.

COURIEL, C.J., and LABARGA, MUÑIZ, GROSSHANS, FRANCIS,
and BARRIOS, JJ., concur.
TANENBAUM, J., concurs in part and dissents in part with an

THE FILING OF A MOTION FOR REHEARING SHALL NOT ALTER

TANENBAUM, J., concurring in part and dissenting in part.

     As this court does not have the authority to regulate non-

lawyers, I dissent from the addition of subdivision (b).
