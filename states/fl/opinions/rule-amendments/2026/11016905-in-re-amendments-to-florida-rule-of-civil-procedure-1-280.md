# In Re: Amendments to Florida Rule of Civil Procedure 1.280

Supreme Court of Florida
                            ____________

                         No. SC2025-1497
                           ____________

       IN RE: AMENDMENTS TO FLORIDA RULE OF CIVIL
                    PROCEDURE 1.280.

                          October 8, 2026

PER CURIAM.

     The Florida Bar’s Civil Procedure Rules Committee filed a

report proposing amendments to Florida Rule of Civil Procedure

1.280 (General Provisions Governing Discovery). 1 The proposed

amendments revise rule 1.280(a) (Initial Discovery Disclosure) to

more closely align with Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 26(a)(1)

(Initial Disclosure). The Committee and the Court separately

published the proposed amendments for comment. No comments

were received in response to either publication.

     The Court, having considered the Committee’s report, hereby

amends rule 1.280 as proposed by the Committee. Under the

     1. We have jurisdiction. See art. V, § 2(a), Fla. Const.; see
also Fla. R. Gen. Prac. & Jud. Admin. 2.140(b).

amended rule, a party is no longer required under subdivision

(a)(1)(C) to provide, along with a computation of each claimed

category of damages, copies of the documents or materials on which

the computation is based. A party will instead now be required to

make those documents or materials available for inspection and

copying under rule 1.350 (Production of Documents and Things and

Entry on Land for Inspection and Other Purposes).

     The Florida Rules of Civil Procedure are amended as set forth

in the appendix to this opinion. New language is indicated by

underscoring; deletions are indicated by struck-through type. The

amendments become effective on January 1, 2027, at 12:01 a.m.

     It is so ordered.

      (i)   Apex Doctrine. A current or former high-level
government or corporate officer may seek an order preventing the
officer from being subject to a deposition. The motion, whether by a
party or by the person of whom the deposition is sought, must be
accompanied by an affidavit or declaration of the officer explaining
