# In Re: Amendments to Florida Rule of Civil Procedure 1.820

Supreme Court of Florida
                            ____________

                         No. SC2026-0040
                           ____________

       IN RE: AMENDMENTS TO FLORIDA RULE OF CIVIL
                    PROCEDURE 1.820.

                         November 12, 2026

PER CURIAM.

     The Florida Bar’s Civil Procedure Rules Committee filed a

report proposing amendments to Florida Rule of Civil Procedure

1.820 (Hearing Procedures for Non-Binding Arbitration). 1 We

published the proposed amendments for comment in The Florida

Bar News. Four comments were received, and the Committee filed

a response. Having considered the Committee’s report, the

comments filed, the response to the comments, and oral argument,

we amend rule 1.820 as proposed by the Committee with some

minor modifications. We discuss the more significant rule

amendments below.

     1. We have jurisdiction. See art. V, § 2(a), Fla. Const.; see
also Fla. R. Gen. Prac. & Jud. Admin. 2.140(b).

     We amend subdivision (g)(3) to remove the requirement that

an arbitration decision and the originals of any accompanying

transcripts be filed under seal with the clerk. An arbitrator will now

file a notice of service of the arbitration decision indicating that the

arbitration decision has been served on the parties. The arbitration

decision must be filed with the court later, either under newly

numbered subdivision (h)(3) by the party entitled to a judgment if

no requests for trial de novo are filed, or under new subdivision (i)

(Time to File the Arbitration Decision After Entry of Judgment) by

the party seeking to enforce the provisions of section 44.103,

Florida Statutes, after a trial de novo.

     We also amend newly numbered subdivision (h)(1) to more

closely align with section 44.103(5), Florida Statutes. Under the

amended rule, a party will need only to file a timely request for trial

de novo to reject an arbitration decision and proceed to a trial.

However, we decline to amend the rule to require that a timely

request for trial de novo be “construed so as to do substantial

justice.” The inclusion of such language could create needless

uncertainty and additional litigation.

                                  -2-

     Accordingly, the Florida Rules of Civil Procedure are amended

as set forth in the appendix to this opinion. New language is

indicated by underscoring; deletions are indicated by struck-

through type. The amendments become effective immediately.

     It is so ordered.

BARRIOS, and TANENBAUM, JJ., concur.

                               APPENDIX

RULE 1.820.      HEARING PROCEDURES FOR NON-BINDING
                 ARBITRATION

     (a) – (f)   [No Change]

     (g)   Completion of the Arbitration Process.

           (1) – (2)   [No Change]

            (3) Within 10 days of the final adjournment of the
arbitration hearing, the arbitrator(s) shallmust notify the parties, in
writing, of their arbitration decision. The arbitration decision may
set forth the issues in controversy, and the arbitrator(’s)(s’)
conclusions, and findings of fact and law. The arbitrator(’s)(s’)
decision and the originals of any transcripts shall be sealed and
filed with the clerk at the time the parties are notified of the
decision.The arbitrator must file a notice of service of the arbitration
decision. The arbitration decision must be filed under either
subdivision (h)(3) or subdivision (i).

           (4)   [No Change]

    (h) Notice of Rejection of the Arbitration Decision and
Request for Trial.

            (1) To reject the arbitration decision, within 20 days of
service of the arbitrator(’s)(s’) written decision, any party must file a
notice of rejection of the arbitration decision and request for trial de
novoin the same document. No action or inaction by any party,
other than the filing of the notice, will be deemed a rejection of the
arbitration decision.

            (2) If a notice of rejection of the arbitration decision and
request for trial de novo is filed by any party, any party having a
third-party claim at issue at the time of arbitration may file a notice
of rejection of the arbitration decision and request for trial de novo
within 10 days of service of the first notice of rejection of the
arbitration decision and request for trial de novo.

                                  -5-

           (3) If a notice of rejection of the arbitration decision and
request for trial de novo is not made within 20 days of service on
the parties of the decision, a party entitled to judgment must file
the arbitration decision.

           (4) Tthe filed arbitration decision must be referred to
the presiding judge, who must enter such orders and judgments as
may be required to carry out the terms of the decision as provided
by section 44.103(5), Florida Statutes.

    (i) Time to File the Arbitration Decision After Entry of
Judgment.

           (1) If a request for trial de novo is filed within 20 days
of the service of the arbitration decision, the arbitration decision
must not be filed until necessary to enforce the provisions of section
44.103, Florida Statutes, after the entry of judgment.

            (2) Within 30 days of the entry of judgment, any party
entitled to a judgment must file and serve the arbitration decision
for the presiding judge to enter any orders and final judgments.
