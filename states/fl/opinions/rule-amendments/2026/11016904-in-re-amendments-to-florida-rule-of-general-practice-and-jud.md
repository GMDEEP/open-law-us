# In Re: Amendments to Florida Rule of General Practice and Judicial Administration 2.270 and Judicial Branch Records Retention Schedule for Administrative Records

Supreme Court of Florida
                             ____________

                          No. SC2025-1414
                            ____________

    IN RE: AMENDMENTS TO FLORIDA RULE OF GENERAL
   PRACTICE AND JUDICIAL ADMINISTRATION 2.270 AND
  JUDICIAL BRANCH RECORDS RETENTION SCHEDULE FOR
               ADMINISTRATIVE RECORDS.

                           October 8, 2026

PER CURIAM.

     The Florida Bar’s Rules of General Practice and Judicial

Administration Committee filed a report proposing amendments to

Florida Rule of General Practice and Judicial Administration 2.270

(Supreme Court Committees on Standard Jury Instructions) and to

the Judicial Branch Records Retention Schedule for Administrative

Records. 1 Both the Committee and the Court published the

proposals for comment; no comments were received.

     We adopt the amendments as proposed. First, rule 2.270 is

amended to provide that all proposed changes to the standard jury

      1. We have jurisdiction. Art. V, § 2(a), Fla. Const.; see also
Fla. R. Gen. Prac. & Jud. Admin. 2.140(b).

instructions, which must be published for comment on The Florida

Bar’s website, may, rather than must, be published in the print

edition of The Florida Bar News, with the same deadline for

comment submission as the website publication.

     The “Search Warrants” section of the retention schedule is

amended to avoid any ambiguity regarding unexecuted search

warrants. And the retention period is revised to one year after the

date of “issuance of the search warrant.”

     Accordingly, we amend the Florida Rules of General Practice

and Judicial Administration and the Judicial Branch Records

Retention Schedule for Administrative Records as reflected in the

appendix to this opinion. New language is underscored; deleted

language is stricken. The amendments shall become effective on

January 1, 2027, at 12:01 a.m.

     It is so ordered.
COURIEL, C.J., and LABARGA, MUÑIZ, GROSSHANS, FRANCIS,
BARRIOS, and TANENBAUM, JJ., concur.
