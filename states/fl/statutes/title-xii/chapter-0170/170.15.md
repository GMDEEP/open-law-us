# § 170.15 Expenditures for improvements.

The governing authority of any municipality may pay out of its general funds or out of any special fund that may be provided for that purpose such portion of the cost of any improvement as it may deem proper.

History: s. 15, ch. 9298, 1923; CGL 3036; s. 12, ch. 59-396.
