# § 165.081 Judicial review.

Any special law or ordinance enacted, and any dismissal of petition made, pursuant to this chapter shall be reviewable by certiorari. No appeal may be brought after the effective date of an incorporation or dissolution.

History: s. 1, ch. 74-192; s. 3, ch. 78-95.
