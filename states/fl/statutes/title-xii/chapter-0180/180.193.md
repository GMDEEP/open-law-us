# § 180.193 Regional utilities authority created by Legislature through charter amendment; preempted to state.

(1) The subject of a regional utilities authority created by the Legislature through charter amendment after January 1, 2023, is expressly preempted to the state.

(2) This section shall take effect upon this act becoming a law.

History: s. 4, ch. 2026-135.
