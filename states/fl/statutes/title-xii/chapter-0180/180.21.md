# § 180.21 Powers granted deemed additional.

The authority and powers granted by this chapter to municipalities shall be in addition to but not in limitation of any of the powers heretofore or hereafter granted to municipalities now existing or hereafter created.

History: s. 15, ch. 17118, 1935; CGL 1936 Supp. 3100(20).
