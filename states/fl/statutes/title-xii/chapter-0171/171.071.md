# § 171.071 Effect in Miami-Dade County.

Municipalities within the boundaries of Miami-Dade County shall adopt annexation or contraction ordinances pursuant to methods established by the home rule charter established pursuant to s. 6(e), Art. VIII of the State Constitution.

History: s. 1, ch. 74-190; s. 31, ch. 2008-4.
