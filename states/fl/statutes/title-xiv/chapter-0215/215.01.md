# § 215.01 Fiscal year.

The fiscal year shall begin on July 1 and end on June 30 in each and every year.

History: s. 5, ch. 515, 1853; RS 405; GS 597; RGS 1032; s. 1, ch. 10124, 1925; s. 1, ch. 10130, 1925; CGL 1343.
