# § 195.0012 Legislative intent.

It is declared to be the legislative purpose and intent in this entire chapter to recognize and fulfill the state’s responsibility to secure a just valuation for ad valorem tax purposes of all property and to provide for a uniform assessment as between property within each county and property in every other county or taxing district.

History: s. 47, ch. 70-243; s. 2, ch. 73-172.

Note. — Former s. 195.111.
