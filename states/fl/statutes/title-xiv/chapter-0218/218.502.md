# § 218.502 Definition.

As used in ss. 218.50-218.504, the term “local governmental entity” means a county, municipality, or special district.

History: s. 8, ch. 79-183; s. 26, ch. 96-324; s. 24, ch. 99-333; s. 34, ch. 2004-305.
