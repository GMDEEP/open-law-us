# § 218.322 County and municipal transportation program data.

Each county and municipality shall annually provide the Department of Transportation with uniform program data. The data must conform to the local governmental entity’s fiscal year and must include, but need not be limited to, details on transportation receipts and expenditures and on the number of miles of road for which the local governmental entity is responsible. The Department of Transportation shall inform each local governmental entity of the method and format for submitting the data. The Department of Transportation shall compile the data and shall furnish the compilation of data to any interested person upon request.

History: s. 29, ch. 96-324.
