# § 207.017 Department to furnish certificate of liens.

The department shall furnish to any person applying therefor a certificate showing the amount of all liens for tax, penalties, and interest that may be of record in the files of the department against any person under the provisions of this chapter.

History: s. 2, ch. 80-415; s. 3, ch. 81-151.

Note. — Former s. 206.988.
