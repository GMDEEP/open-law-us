# § 210.03 Prohibition against levying of cigarette taxes by municipalities.

No municipality shall levy or collect any excise tax on cigarettes.

History: s. 1, ch. 26320, 1949; s. 1, ch. 63-486; ss. 16, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 10, ch. 72-360; s. 35, ch. 2019-3.
