# § 206.945 Settlement or compromise of tax, penalty, or interest.

The department may settle or compromise tax, penalty, or interest imposed by this part pursuant to s. 213.21.

History: s. 8, ch. 81-178; s. 65, ch. 83-217; s. 94, ch. 90-136; s. 6, ch. 90-351.
