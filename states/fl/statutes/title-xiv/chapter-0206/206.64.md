# § 206.64 Refunds on fuel used for agricultural or commercial fishing purposes.

Any person who uses any motor fuel for agricultural purposes or commercial fishing purposes on which the tax, as imposed by this part, has been paid shall be entitled to a refund of the municipal tax imposed by s. 206.41(1)(c) according to the administrative procedures in s. 206.41.
