# § 217.01 Purpose.

The purpose of this chapter is to provide authority to a designated state agency for the procurement and distribution of federal surplus personal property for public agencies; for eligible nonprofit, tax-exempt educational and health organizations; and for eligible nonprofit, tax-exempt organizations that assist the homeless.

History: s. 1, ch. 65-173; s. 9, ch. 83-334; s. 161, ch. 92-279; s. 55, ch. 92-326.
