# § 217.13 Officers and employees.

The department is authorized to appoint or employ such agents, officers or employees as it shall deem necessary for carrying out the provisions of this chapter.

History: s. 1, ch. 65-173; ss. 22, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 70, ch. 73-333.
