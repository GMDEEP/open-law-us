# § 212.19 All state agencies to cooperate in administration of law.

The department is further empowered to call on any state agency, department, bureau or board for any and all information which may, in its judgment, be of assistance in administering or preparing for the administration of this chapter, and such state agency, department, bureau or board is hereby authorized, directed and required to furnish such information.
