# § 220.701 Collection authority.

The department shall collect the taxes imposed by this chapter and shall pay all moneys received by it into the General Revenue Fund of the state.

History: s. 19, ch. 71-359; s. 43, ch. 91-112.

Note. — Former s. 214.02.
