# § 220.711 Notice of deficiency.

A notice of deficiency issued under this chapter shall set forth, in addition to the amount of tax and any penalties, a computation of the adjustments giving rise to the proposed assessment and the reason or reasons therefor.

History: s. 19, ch. 71-359; s. 48, ch. 91-112.

Note. — Former s. 214.07.
