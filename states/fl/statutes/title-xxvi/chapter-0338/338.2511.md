# § 338.2511 Deposit and use of funds in Toll Facilities Revolving Trust Fund.

The funds in the Toll Facilities Revolving Trust Fund and all future payments of obligated funds shall be deposited into the State Transportation Trust Fund to be expended for the purposes specified in s. 339.08.

History: s. 41, ch. 2012-128.
