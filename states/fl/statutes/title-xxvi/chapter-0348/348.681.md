# § 348.681 Design standards.

The geometric design standards used in connection with constructing the expressway system or any extension thereof shall as nearly as is practicable follow or be superior to design standards adopted for the National System of Interstate and Defense Highways.

History: s. 1, ch. 65-555; s. 1, ch. 65-556.
