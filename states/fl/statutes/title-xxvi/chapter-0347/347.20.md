# § 347.20 Vested rights not impaired.

Nothing in this chapter shall affect or impair any right or privilege belonging to any individual or corporation by virtue of any law of this state.

History: s. 10, ch. 3039, 1877; RS 645; GS 918; RGS 1702; CGL 2753.
