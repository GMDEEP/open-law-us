# § 334.17 Consulting services; provision by department to other governmental units.

The department is authorized to provide consulting services, upon request, to any governmental unit on such terms as may be mutually agreed upon in writing.

History: s. 16, ch. 29965, 1955; ss. 23, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 18, ch. 84-309.
