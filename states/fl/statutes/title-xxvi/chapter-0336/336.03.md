# § 336.03 County engineer; qualifications.

The county engineer must be a registered professional engineer or engineering firm qualified to do business in this state. This does not apply to any county engineer who:

(1) Was employed on or before June 30, 1967;

(2) Was employed on less than a full-time basis; and

(3) Was not employed to furnish professional engineering advice on road programs in the county.
