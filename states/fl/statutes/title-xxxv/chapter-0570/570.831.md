# § 570.831 Florida beef marketing program.

The Cattle Enhancement Board, Inc., in coordination with the department, shall, subject to appropriation, establish a Florida beef marketing program to conduct research designed to expand the uses of beef and beef products and strengthen the market position of Florida’s cattle industry through marketing campaigns and promotions within this state and the nation.

History: s. 66, ch. 2025-22.
