# § 570.031 Seal of department.

The department shall have an official seal which shall be used for the authentication of the orders and proceedings of the department and for such other purposes as the department may prescribe. Use of the seal or any likeness thereof requires written approval of the department.

History: s. 1, ch. 59-54; ss. 14, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 10, ch. 93-169; s. 69, ch. 2014-150.

Note. — Former s. 570.14.
