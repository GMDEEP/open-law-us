# § 601.501 Charitable shipments exempt from assessments.

Shipments of citrus fruit when permitted under s. 601.50 for charitable purposes are exempt from all advertising assessments.

History: s. 3, ch. 28197, 1953; s. 38, ch. 2012-182.
