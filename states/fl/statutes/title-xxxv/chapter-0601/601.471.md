# § 601.471 Definition of “canned or concentrated citrus fruit products” expanded.

The term “canned or concentrated citrus fruit products” when used in ss. 601.48-601.54 shall include chilled citrus juice, chilled citrus sections, or otherwise processed products of citrus fruit.

History: s. 1, ch. 65-72.
