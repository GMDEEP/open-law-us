# § 601.85 Standard shipping box for fresh fruit.

The specifications for the standard shipping box, when used as a unit of trade or for reporting purposes, shall be as established by the department, but the unit of a standard-packed box, commonly called 1 3 / 5 bushels, shall contain an inside cubical measurement of 3,456 cubic inches.

History: s. 85, ch. 25149, 1949; s. 22, ch. 71-186; s. 62, ch. 2012-182.
