# § 583.06 Employment of assistants by department.

The department may employ such assistants as are necessary to carry out and enforce the provisions of this chapter.

History: s. 12, ch. 16982, 1935; CGL 1936 Supp. 4126(12); s. 2, ch. 61-119; ss. 14, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 9, ch. 84-102.
