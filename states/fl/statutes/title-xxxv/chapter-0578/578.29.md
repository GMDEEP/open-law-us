# § 578.29 Prohibited noxious weed seed.

Seeds meeting the definition of prohibited noxious weed seed under s. 578.011 may not be present in agricultural, vegetable, flower, tree, or shrub seed offered or exposed for sale in this state.

History: s. 54, ch. 2018-84.
