# § 736.08105 Duty to ascertain marketable title of trust real property.

A trustee holding title to real property received from a settlor or estate shall not be required to obtain title insurance or proof of marketable title until a marketable title is required for a sale or conveyance of the real property.

History: s. 8, ch. 2006-217.
