# § 736.0112 Qualification of foreign trustee.

Unless otherwise doing business in this state, local qualification by a foreign trustee is not required for the trustee to receive distribution from a local estate. Nothing in this chapter shall affect the provisions of s. 660.41.

History: s. 1, ch. 2006-217.
