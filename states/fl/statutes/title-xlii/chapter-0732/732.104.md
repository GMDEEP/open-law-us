# § 732.104 Inheritance per stirpes.

Descent shall be per stirpes, whether to descendants or to collateral heirs.

History: s. 1, ch. 74-106; s. 9, ch. 75-220; s. 7, ch. 2007-74.

Note. — Created from former s. 731.25.
