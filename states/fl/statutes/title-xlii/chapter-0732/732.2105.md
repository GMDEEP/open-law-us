# § 732.2105 Effect of election on other interests.

The elective share shall be in addition to homestead, exempt property, and allowances as provided in part IV.

History: s. 15, ch. 75-220; s. 10, ch. 99-343; s. 26, ch. 2001-226.

Note. — Former s. 732.208.
