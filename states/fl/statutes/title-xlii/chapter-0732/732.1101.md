# § 732.1101 Aliens.

Aliens shall have the same rights of inheritance as citizens.

History: s. 1, ch. 74-106; s. 113, ch. 75-220; s. 955, ch. 97-102; s. 18, ch. 2001-226.

Note. — Created from former s. 731.28.
