# § 243.40 Provisions of part controlling.

To the extent that the provisions of this part are inconsistent with the provisions of any general statute or special act or parts thereof, the provisions of this part shall be deemed controlling.

History: s. 23, ch. 69-345; s. 1, ch. 77-174.
