# § 115.06 Reassumption of duties.

Upon being mustered out of the service of the United States, such officer granted leave under s. 115.01 shall immediately enter into the duties of his or her office for the remainder of the term for which he or she was elected.

History: s. 6, ch. 7393, 1917; RGS 405; CGL 470; s. 736, ch. 95-147; s. 9, ch. 2026-92.
