# § 122.26 Funds.

There shall be paid into the State and County Officers and Employees’ Retirement Trust Fund, provided in former s. 122.17, contributions by members of division B for benefits payable to members under this system, and all amounts appropriated for such purpose by the state.

History: s. 2, ch. 57-382; s. 2, ch. 61-119; s. 13, ch. 2004-234.
