# § 113.02 Fee to be paid before commissions issued.

No commission shall be issued by the Governor or attested by the Secretary of State or shall bear the seal of the state until the fee fixed and required by s. 113.01, if any, shall first be paid as therein provided.

History: s. 2, ch. 14669, 1931; CGL 1936 Supp. 460(2); s. 2, ch. 81-260.
