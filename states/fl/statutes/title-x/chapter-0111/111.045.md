# § 111.045 Salaries of officers payable upon requisition.

The salary of every officer shall be payable monthly upon his or her own requisition.

History: Former s. 3, Art. XVI of the State Constitution of 1885, as amended, converted to statutory law by s. 10, Art. XII of the State Constitution as revised in 1968; see Flack v. Graham , 453 So. 2d 819 (Fla. 1984); s. 674, ch. 95-147.
