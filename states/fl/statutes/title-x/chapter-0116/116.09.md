# § 116.09 Penalty for failure.

Any officer who shall neglect or refuse to comply with the duties imposed by s. 116.07 shall be subject to suspension from office by the Governor.

History: s. 4, ch. 5176, 1903; GS 817; RGS 412; CGL 477.
