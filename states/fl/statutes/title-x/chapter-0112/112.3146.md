# § 112.3146 Public records.

The statements required by ss. 112.313, 112.3145, 112.3148, and 112.3149 shall be public records within the meaning of s. 119.01.

History: s. 6, ch. 74-177; s. 6, ch. 90-502; s. 7, ch. 91-85.
