# § 112.1913 Effect of ch. 2003-412.

The amendments to ss. 440.02 and 440.15 which are made by this act shall not be construed to affect any determination of disability under s. 112.18, s. 112.181, or s. 112.19.

History: s. 48, ch. 2003-412.
