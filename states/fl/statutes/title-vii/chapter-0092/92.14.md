# § 92.14 United States deeds and patents and copies thereof.

Deeds and patents issued by the United States Government and photographic copies made by authority of said government from its records thereof in the General Land Office, embracing lands in this state, and certified copies of the record thereof made in this state may be used in evidence in the courts of this state subject to the same rules that are applicable to the admission in evidence of other deeds and certified copies of the record thereof.

History: s. 3, ch. 8565, 1921; CGL 5716.
