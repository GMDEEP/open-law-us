# § 368.1085 Travel costs.

The commission has the authority to assess a natural gas transmission company for reasonable travel costs associated with reviewing the records of the natural gas transmission company and its affiliates when such records are kept out of state. The natural gas transmission company may bring the records back into the state for review.

History: s. 14, ch. 93-35.
