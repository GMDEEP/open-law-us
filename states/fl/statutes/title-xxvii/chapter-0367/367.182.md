# § 367.182 Saving clause.

All certificates and authorizations valid on the effective date of chapter 80-99, Laws of Florida, shall remain in full force and effect. Henceforth, all certificates and authorizations shall be applied for in accordance with this act.

History: s. 24, ch. 80-99; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 24, 26, 27, ch. 89-353; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
