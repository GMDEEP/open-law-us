# § 367.1214 Utility name change; notification; commission rulemaking authority.

A water and wastewater utility shall notify the commission and its customers before changing its name. The commission may adopt rules that a water and wastewater utility must follow when giving this notice to its customers.

History: s. 3, ch. 98-42.
