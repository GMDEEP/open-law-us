# § 366.13 Taxes, not affected.

No provision of this chapter shall in any way affect any municipal tax or franchise tax in any manner whatsoever.

History: s. 13A, ch. 26545, 1951; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; s. 16, ch. 80-35; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 20, 22, ch. 89-292; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
