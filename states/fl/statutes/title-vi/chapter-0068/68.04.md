# § 68.04 Chancery jurisdiction over liens.

All liens of any kind, whether created by statute or the common law, and whether heretofore regarded as merely possessory or not, may be enforced in chancery.

History: RS 1510; GS 1960; RGS 3228; CGL 5034; s. 22, ch. 67-254.

Note. — Former s. 62.36.
