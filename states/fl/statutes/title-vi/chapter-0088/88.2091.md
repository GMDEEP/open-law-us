# § 88.2091 Credit for payments.

A tribunal of this state shall credit amounts collected for a particular period pursuant to any child support order against the amounts owed for the same period under any other child support order for support of the same child issued by the tribunal of this state, another state, or a foreign country.

History: s. 2, ch. 96-189; s. 13, ch. 2011-92.
