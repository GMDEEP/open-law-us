# § 66.051 Betterment, answer.

The plaintiff in the judgment of eviction may file written defenses to the petition within 20 days after service of the petition.

History: RS 1517; GS 1972; RGS 3240; CGL 5048; s. 14, ch. 29737, 1955; s. 21, ch. 67-254.

Note. — Former s. 70.07.
