# § 60.06 Abatement of nuisances; enforcement.

The court shall make such orders on proper proof as will abate all nuisances mentioned in s. 823.05, and has authority to enforce injunctions by contempt but the jurisdiction hereby granted does not repeal or alter s. 823.01.

History: s. 5, ch. 7367, 1917; RGS 3227; CGL 5033; s. 15, ch. 67-254.

Note. — Former s. 64.15.
