# § 83.251 Removal of tenant; costs.

The prevailing party shall have judgment for costs and execution shall issue therefor.

History: s. 11, ch. 6463, 1913; RGS 3552; CGL 5416; s. 34, ch. 67-254.

Note. — Former s. 83.37.
