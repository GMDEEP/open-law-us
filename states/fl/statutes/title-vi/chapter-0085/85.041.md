# § 85.041 Joinder.

All persons who have liens under part I or part II of chapter 713, may join to enforce their respective liens.

History: s. 14, ch. 5143, 1903; GS 2224; RGS 3531; CGL 5394; s. 37, ch. 67-254.

Note. — Former s. 86.10.
