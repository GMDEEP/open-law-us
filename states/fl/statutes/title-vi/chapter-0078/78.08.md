# § 78.08 Writ; form.

The writ shall command the sheriff to replevy the described personal property in possession of defendant.

History: s. 4, Mar. 11, 1845; RS 1714; GS 2178; RGS 3483; CGL 5336; s. 28, ch. 67-254; s. 1, ch. 73-20; s. 6, ch. 83-255.
