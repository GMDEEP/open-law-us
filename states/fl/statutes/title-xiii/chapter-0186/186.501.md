# § 186.501 Short title.

Sections 186.501-186.513 shall be known and may be cited as the “Florida Regional Planning Council Act.”

History: ss. 1, 5, ch. 80-315; s. 4, ch. 82-46; s. 1, ch. 92-182; s. 38, ch. 93-206.

Note. — Former s. 160.001.
