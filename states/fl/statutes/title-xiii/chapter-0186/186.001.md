# § 186.001 Short title.

Sections 186.001-186.031 and 186.801-186.901 shall be known and may be cited as the “Florida State Comprehensive Planning Act of 1972.”

History: s. 1, ch. 72-295; s. 68, ch. 99-2.

Note. — Former s. 23.0111.
