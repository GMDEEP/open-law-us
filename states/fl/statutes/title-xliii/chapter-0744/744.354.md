# § 744.354 Validity of bond.

No bond executed by any guardian shall be invalid because of an informality in it or because of an informality or illegality in the appointment of the guardian. The bond shall have the same force and effect as if the bond had been executed in proper form and the appointment had been legally made.

History: s. 1, ch. 74-106; ss. 19, 26, ch. 75-222; s. 42, ch. 89-96.
