# § 744.1025 Additional definitions.

The definitions contained in the Florida Probate Code shall be applicable to the Florida Guardianship Law, unless the context requires otherwise, insofar as such definitions do not conflict with definitions contained in this law.

History: s. 2, ch. 79-221; s. 5, ch. 89-96.
