# § 744.609 Procedure for commitment of veteran to United States Department of Veterans Affairs hospital.

The procedure for the placement into a United States Department of Veterans Affairs hospital of a ward hereunder shall be the procedure prescribed in s. 394.4672.

History: s. 22, ch. 84-62; s. 41, ch. 93-268.
