# § 744.381 Appraisals.

When the court deems it necessary, appraisers may be appointed to appraise the property of the ward that is subject to the guardianship.

History: s. 1, ch. 74-106; s. 61, ch. 89-96; s. 46, ch. 90-271.

Note. — Created from former s. 744.54.
