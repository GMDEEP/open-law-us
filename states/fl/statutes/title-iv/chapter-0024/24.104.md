# § 24.104 Department; purpose.

The purpose of the department is to operate the state lottery as authorized by s. 15, Art. X of the State Constitution so as to maximize revenues in a manner consonant with the dignity of the state and the welfare of its citizens.

History: s. 4, ch. 87-65; s. 6, ch. 2000-158.
