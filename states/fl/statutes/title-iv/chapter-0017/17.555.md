# § 17.555 Division of Treasury to keep record of warrants and of state funds and securities.

The Division of Treasury shall keep a record of the warrants or other orders of the Chief Financial Officer which the Division of Treasury pays and shall account for all state funds and securities.

History: s. 4, ch. 3563, 1885; RS 124; GS 129; RGS 140; CGL 170; s. 3, ch. 73-266; s. 4, ch. 91-244; s. 72, ch. 95-147; s. 1, ch. 95-280; s. 52, ch. 2003-261.

Note. — Former s. 18.07.
