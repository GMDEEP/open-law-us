# § 17.16 Seal.

The office of the Chief Financial Officer shall have an official seal by which its proceedings are authenticated.

History: s. 7, ch. 8, 1845; RS 108; GS 112; RGS 124; CGL 154; s. 32, ch. 2003-261; s. 2, ch. 2004-390.
