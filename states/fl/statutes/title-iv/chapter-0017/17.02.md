# § 17.02 Place of residence and office.

The Chief Financial Officer shall reside at the seat of government of this state and shall hold office in a room in the capitol.

History: s. 2, ch. 8, 1845; ch. 1845, 1871; RS 94; GS 98; RGS 111; CGL 141; s. 53, ch. 95-147; s. 15, ch. 2003-261.
