# § 17.62 Interest on state moneys deposited; when paid.

Interest on state moneys deposited in qualified public depositories under s. 17.57 shall be payable to the Chief Financial Officer quarterly.

History: s. 4, ch. 4586, 1897; GS 136; RGS 147; CGL 177; s. 1, ch. 65-99; ss. 28, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 5, ch. 81-295; s. 5, ch. 83-122; s. 3, ch. 96-177; s. 62, ch. 2003-261.

Note. — Former s. 18.15.
