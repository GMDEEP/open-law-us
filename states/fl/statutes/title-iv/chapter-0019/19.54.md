# § 19.54 Nathan Mayo Building.

The name of the state chemistry building in Tallahassee, Florida, is designated and declared to be “The Nathan Mayo Building,” in tribute to the Honorable Nathan Mayo, Commissioner of Agriculture of the state.

History: s. 1, ch. 22590, 1945.
