# § 8.02 New counties.

When any new counties are created, such new counties shall become a part of the congressional district in which the territory for such new county is located.

History: s. 6, ch. 6472, 1913; RGS 69; CGL 85; s. 7, ch. 16876, 1935; s. 8, ch. 21975, 1943; s. 10, ch. 26484, 1951.
