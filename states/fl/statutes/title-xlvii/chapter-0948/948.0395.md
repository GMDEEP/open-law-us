# § 948.0395 Probation conditions for defendants with mental illness.

A defendant who was adjudicated incompetent to proceed due to a mental illness under chapter 916 and later regained competency, and who is sentenced to a term of probation, must have as a condition of such probation a mental health evaluation and must follow all recommendations of the evaluation.

History: s. 8, ch. 2025-180.
