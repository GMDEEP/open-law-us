# § 924.35 Enforcement of judgment on affirmance.

When the judgment against the defendant is affirmed, the judgment shall be enforced by the trial court.

History: s. 311, ch. 19554, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 8663(324); s. 162, ch. 70-339.
