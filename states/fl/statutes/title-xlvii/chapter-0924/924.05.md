# § 924.05 Appeal as matter of right.

Direct appeals provided for in this chapter are a matter of right.

History: s. 284, ch. 19554, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 8663(294); s. 146, ch. 70-339; s. 3, ch. 96-248.
