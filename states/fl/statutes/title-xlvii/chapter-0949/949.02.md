# § 949.02 Youth parolees.

Nothing in chapters 947-949 shall be construed to change or modify the law respecting paroles as administered by the Department of Juvenile Justice.

History: s. 31, ch. 20519, 1941; s. 1, ch. 57-317; ss. 19, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 477, ch. 77-147; s. 324, ch. 99-8.
