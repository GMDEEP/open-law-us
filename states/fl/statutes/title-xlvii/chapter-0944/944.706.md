# § 944.706 Basic release assistance.

(1) Any inmate who is being released is eligible for transition assistance. Those inmates released to a detainer are eligible pursuant to s. 944.703.

(2) The department may contract with the Department of Children and Families, the Salvation Army, and other public or private organizations, including faith-based service groups, for the provision of basic support services for releasees.

(3) The department shall adopt rules for the development, implementation, and termination of transition assistance.
