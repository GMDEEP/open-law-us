# § 944.7065 Transition course for inmates.

In an effort to ensure that inmates released from the Department of Corrections successfully reenter the community, beginning December 1, 2002, each inmate released from incarceration by the department must complete a 100-hour comprehensive transition course that covers job readiness and life management skills. This requirement does not apply to inmates released in an emergency situation.

History: s. 20, ch. 2001-110.
