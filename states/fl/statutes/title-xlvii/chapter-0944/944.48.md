# § 944.48 Service of sentence.

Whenever any prisoner is convicted under the provisions of ss. 944.44-944.47, the punishment of imprisonment imposed shall be served consecutively to any former sentence imposed upon any prisoner convicted hereunder.

History: s. 8, ch. 57-313; s. 96, ch. 2018-110.
