# § 944.52 Legal adviser.

The Department of Legal Affairs shall be the legal adviser of the Department of Corrections.

History: s. 42, ch. 57-121; s. 18, ch. 61-530; ss. 11, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 63, ch. 77-120; s. 74, ch. 79-3.
