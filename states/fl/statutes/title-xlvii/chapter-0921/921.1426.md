# § 921.1426 Sentence of death for capital offense committed by unauthorized alien.

Notwithstanding any provision of law to the contrary, the court shall sentence a defendant who is an unauthorized alien and who is convicted or adjudicated guilty of a capital felony to a sentence of death. As used in this section, the term “unauthorized alien” has the same meaning as in s. 908.111.

History: s. 5, ch. 2025-2.
