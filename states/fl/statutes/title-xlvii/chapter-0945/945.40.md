# § 945.40 Corrections Mental Health Act; short title for ss. 945.40-945.49.

Sections 945.40-945.49 shall be known and may be cited as the “Corrections Mental Health Act.”

History: s. 1, ch. 82-224; s. 29, ch. 84-361.
