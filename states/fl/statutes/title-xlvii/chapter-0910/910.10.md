# § 910.10 Where stolen property brought into another county.

A person who obtains property by larceny, robbery, or embezzlement may be tried in any county in which the person exercises control over the property.

History: s. 169, ch. 19554, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 8663(176); s. 80, ch. 70-339; s. 1516, ch. 97-102.
