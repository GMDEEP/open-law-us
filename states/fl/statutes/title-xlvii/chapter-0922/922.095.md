# § 922.095 Pursuit of collateral remedies.

A person who is convicted and sentenced to death must pursue all possible collateral remedies in state court in accordance with the Florida Rules of Criminal Procedure.

History: s. 7, ch. 96-290; s. 112, ch. 99-3; s. 4, ch. 2000-3; s. 11, ch. 2013-216.
