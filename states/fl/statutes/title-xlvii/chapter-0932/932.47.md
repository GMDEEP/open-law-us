# § 932.47 Informations filed by prosecuting attorneys.

Informations may be filed by the prosecuting attorney of the circuit court with the clerk of the circuit court without leave of the court first being obtained.

History: s. 1, ch. 17172, 1935; CGL 1936 Supp. 8363(1); s. 24, ch. 2013-25.
