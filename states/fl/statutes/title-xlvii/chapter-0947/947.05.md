# § 947.05 Seal.

The commission shall adopt an official seal of which the courts shall take judicial notice.

History: s. 3, ch. 20519, 1941; s. 34, ch. 83-131; s. 37, ch. 86-183; s. 67, ch. 88-122; s. 17, ch. 89-531; s. 20, ch. 90-337; s. 1, ch. 93-2.
