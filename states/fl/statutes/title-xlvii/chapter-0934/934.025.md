# § 934.025 Search warrants and court orders; remote appearance authorized.

A judge may authorize a law enforcement officer applying for a search warrant or court order pursuant to this chapter to appear remotely using audio-video communication technology. As used in this section, the term “audio-video communication technology” has the same meaning as in s. 117.201.

History: s. 4, ch. 2026-177.
