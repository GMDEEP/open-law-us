# § 905.09 Discharge and recall of grand jury.

A grand jury that has been dismissed may be recalled at any time during the term of the grand jury.

History: s. 88, ch. 19554, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 8663(88); s. 48, ch. 70-339; s. 20, ch. 2013-25.
