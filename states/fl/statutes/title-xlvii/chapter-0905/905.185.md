# § 905.185 State attorney to issue process.

When requested by the grand jury, the state attorney or his or her designated assistant shall issue process to secure the attendance of witnesses.

History: s. 20, ch. 1628, 1868; RS 2807; GS 3856; RGS 5951; CGL 8217; s. 56, ch. 70-339; s. 1498, ch. 97-102.

Note. — Former s. 932.17.
