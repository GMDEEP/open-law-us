# § 771.07 Penalties.

Any person who violates any of the provisions of this chapter shall be guilty of a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

History: s. 7, ch. 23138, 1945; s. 698, ch. 71-136.
