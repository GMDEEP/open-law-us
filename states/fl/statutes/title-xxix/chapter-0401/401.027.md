# § 401.027 Federal assistance.

The Secretary of Management Services or his or her designee is authorized to apply for and accept federal funding assistance in the development and implementation of a statewide emergency medical telecommunications system.

History: s. 6, ch. 73-254; s. 52, ch. 99-399.
