# § 401.2916 Educational campaign.

The Department of Health shall implement an educational campaign to inform any person who acquires an automated external defibrillator device of the scope and limitations of the immunity from liability provided under s. 768.1325.

History: s. 5, ch. 2006-125; s. 2, ch. 2006-206.
