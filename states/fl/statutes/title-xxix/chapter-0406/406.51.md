# § 406.51 Final disposition of unclaimed deceased veterans; contract requirements.

Any contract by a local governmental entity for the final disposition of unclaimed remains must provide for compliance with s. 406.50(2) and require that the procedures in 38 C.F.R. s. 38.620, relating to disposition of unclaimed deceased veterans, are followed.

History: s. 2, ch. 2002-204; s. 43, ch. 2003-1; s. 7, ch. 2013-138.
