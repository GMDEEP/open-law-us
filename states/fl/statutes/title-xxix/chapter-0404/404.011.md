# § 404.011 Short title.

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the “Florida Radiation Protection Act.”

History: s. 1, ch. 78-373; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 17, 18, ch. 84-190; s. 4, ch. 91-429.

Note. — Former s. 290.011.
