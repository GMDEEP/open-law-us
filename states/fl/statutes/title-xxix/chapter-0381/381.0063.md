# § 381.0063 Drinking water funds.

All fees and penalties received from suppliers of water pursuant to ss. 403.860(5) and 403.861(7)(a) shall be deposited in the appropriate County Health Department Trust Fund to be used by the department to pay the costs of expenditures required pursuant to ss. 381.0062 and 403.862(1)(c).

History: s. 69, ch. 90-331; s. 24, ch. 91-297; s. 21, ch. 91-305; s. 38, ch. 97-101; s. 45, ch. 2009-21.

Note. — Former s. 381.2615.
