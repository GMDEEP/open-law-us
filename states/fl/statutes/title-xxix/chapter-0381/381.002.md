# § 381.002 Grant of title to prescriptive medical personal property to client.

Legal title to prescriptive medical personal property purchased by the department for the use or benefit of a particular client of the department may, pursuant to rules adopted by the department, be granted to such client. Such property is not subject to the requirements of chapter 273.

History: s. 1, ch. 85-186; s. 12, ch. 91-297.

Note. — Former s. 381.213.
