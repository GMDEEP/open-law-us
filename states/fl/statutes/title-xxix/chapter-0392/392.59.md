# § 392.59 Forms to be developed.

The department shall develop and furnish to the court all forms necessary under ss. 392.55, 392.56, 392.565, and 392.57, and the court may use such forms where appropriate.

History: s. 1, ch. 88-389; s. 1, ch. 88-398; s. 10, ch. 94-320.
