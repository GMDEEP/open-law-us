# § 408.832 Conflicts.

In case of conflict between this part and the authorizing statutes governing the licensure of health care providers by the Agency for Health Care Administration found in s. 112.0455 and chapters 383, 390, 394, 395, 400, 429, 440, and 765, this part shall prevail.

History: s. 10, ch. 2006-192; s. 93, ch. 2007-5; s. 133, ch. 2007-230; s. 35, ch. 2020-156.
