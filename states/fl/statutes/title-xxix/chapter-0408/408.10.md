# § 408.10 Consumer complaints.

The agency shall publish and make available to the public a toll-free telephone number for the purpose of handling consumer complaints and shall serve as a liaison between consumer entities and other private entities and governmental entities for the disposition of problems identified by consumers of health care.

History: s. 76, ch. 92-33; s. 22, ch. 2012-160.
