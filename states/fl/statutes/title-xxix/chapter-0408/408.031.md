# § 408.031 Short title.

Sections 408.031-408.045 shall be known and may be cited as the “Health Facility and Services Development Act.”

History: s. 18, ch. 87-92; s. 15, ch. 92-33; s. 7, ch. 95-144.

Note. — Former s. 381.701.
