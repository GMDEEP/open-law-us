# § 403.9413 Amendment to the application.

(1) Any amendment made to the application shall be sent by the applicant to the administrative law judge and to all parties to the proceeding.

(2) Any amendment to the application made prior to certification shall be disposed of as part of the original certification proceeding. Amendment of the application may be considered good cause for alteration of time limits pursuant to s. 403.9414.

History: s. 1, ch. 92-284; s. 178, ch. 96-410.
