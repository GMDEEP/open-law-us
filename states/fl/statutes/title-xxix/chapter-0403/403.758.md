# § 403.758 Enforcement and penalty.

(1) Except as provided in subsection (2), the department may enforce ss. 403.75-403.769 and s. 526.01, as amended by chapter 84-338, Laws of Florida, pursuant to ss. 403.121, 403.131, and 403.161.

(2) Any person who fails to register with the department as required by ss. 403.754 and 526.01, as amended by chapter 84-338, Laws of Florida, is subject to a fine of $300.

History: s. 63, ch. 84-338; s. 31, ch. 88-130; s. 50, ch. 93-207.
