# § 394.4995 Conversion of specified facilities to children’s behavioral crisis units; not required.

Nothing in 1 s. 394.499 shall be construed to require an existing crisis stabilization unit or juvenile addictions receiving facility to convert to a children’s behavioral crisis unit.

History: s. 7, ch. 2001-171; s. 2, ch. 2001-191.

1 Note. — As enacted by s. 2, ch. 2001-191. Section 394.4995 was also enacted by s. 7, ch. 2001-171, and that version references “this act” instead of specifying s. 394.499.
