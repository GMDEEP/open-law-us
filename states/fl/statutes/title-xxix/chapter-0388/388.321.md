# § 388.321 Equipment to become property of a program.

All equipment purchased under this chapter with state funds made available directly to a program shall become the property of the program unless otherwise provided, and may be traded in on other equipment, or sold, when no longer needed by the program.

History: s. 2, ch. 59-195; s. 1, ch. 63-236; s. 19, ch. 2025-22.
