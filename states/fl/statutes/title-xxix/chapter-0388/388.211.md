# § 388.211 Change in district boundaries.

The boundaries of each district may only be changed by a special act of the Legislature.

History: s. 2, ch. 59-195; s. 1, ch. 63-236; s. 3, ch. 71-323; s. 11, ch. 80-281; s. 11, ch. 2024-136.
