# § 402.12 National Community Mental Health Centers Act.

Any federal funds accruing to the state for the purposes of carrying out the national Community Mental Health Centers Act of 1963 shall be paid to the Department of Children and Families for expenditure as directed by said department.

History: s. 1, ch. 63-305; ss. 19, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 262, ch. 77-147; s. 132, ch. 99-8; s. 131, ch. 2014-19.

Note. — Former s. 965.16.
