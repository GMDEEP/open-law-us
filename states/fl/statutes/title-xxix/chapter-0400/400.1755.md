# § 400.1755 Care for persons with Alzheimer’s disease or related disorders; staff training requirements.

As a condition of licensure, the employees of facilities licensed under this part must complete the training required under s. 430.5025.

History: s. 26, ch. 2001-45; s. 62, ch. 2005-2; s. 3, ch. 2023-278.
