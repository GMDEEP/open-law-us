# § 629.031 Scope of chapter.

All authorized reciprocal insurers shall be governed by those sections of this chapter not expressly made applicable to domestic reciprocal insurers.

History: s. 673, ch. 59-205; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 667, 681, 809(1st), ch. 82-243; s. 123, ch. 83-216; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
