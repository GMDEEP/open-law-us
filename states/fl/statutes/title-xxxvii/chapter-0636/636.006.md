# § 636.006 Insurance business not authorized.

Nothing in the Florida Insurance Code or this act authorizes any prepaid limited health service organization to transact any insurance business other than that specifically authorized by this act, or otherwise to engage in any other type of insurance unless it is authorized under a certificate of authority issued by the office under the provisions of the Florida Insurance Code.

History: s. 5, ch. 93-148; s. 1524, ch. 2003-261.
