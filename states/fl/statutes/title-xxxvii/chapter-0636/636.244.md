# § 636.244 Unlicensed discount plan organizations.

Sections 626.901-626.912 apply to the activities of an unlicensed discount plan organization as if the unlicensed discount plan organization were an unauthorized insurer.

History: s. 31, ch. 2004-297; s. 30, ch. 2017-112.
