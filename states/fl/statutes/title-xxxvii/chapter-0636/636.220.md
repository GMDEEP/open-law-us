# § 636.220 Minimum capital requirements.

(1) Each discount plan organization shall at all times maintain a net worth of at least $150,000.

(2) The office may not issue a license unless the discount plan organization has a net worth of at least $150,000.

History: s. 31, ch. 2004-297; s. 21, ch. 2017-112.
