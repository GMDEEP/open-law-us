# § 636.057 Fees.

Every prepaid limited health service organization subject to this act must pay to the office the following fees:

(1) For filing an application for a certificate of authority or amendment thereto: $500.

(2) For filing each annual report: $200.

(3) For each renewal of certificate of authority: $500.

History: s. 46, ch. 93-148; s. 1548, ch. 2003-261.
