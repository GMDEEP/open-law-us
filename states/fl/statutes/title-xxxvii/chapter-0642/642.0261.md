# § 642.0261 Net worth required of applicants.

To receive authority to transact legal expense insurance, an applicant applying for a certificate of authority after October 1, 1993, must possess a net worth not less than the greater of:

(1) Ten thousand dollars; or

(2) Ten percent of the applicant’s total liabilities.

History: ss. 3, 7, ch. 93-147.
