# § 642.019 Organization of legal expense insurance corporations.

(1) Any number of corporations or adult natural persons may organize a legal expense insurance corporation under the law of this state relating to corporations generally.

(2) The articles of incorporation shall conform to the requirements applicable to corporations, and, in addition:

(a) The name of the corporation shall indicate that legal services or indemnity for legal expenses is to be provided; and

(b) The purposes of the corporation shall be limited to providing legal services or indemnity for legal expenses and business reasonably related thereto.
