# § 624.487 Enforcement of specified insurance provisions.

The office may enforce, with respect to group self-insurance funds established or operated under s. 624.4621, the provisions of s. 624.316, s. 624.424, s. 625.091, or s. 625.305 as they relate to workers’ compensation insurers.

History: s. 132, ch. 91-108; s. 91, ch. 93-415; s. 838, ch. 2003-261; s. 93, ch. 2013-18.

Note. — Former s. 440.5705.
