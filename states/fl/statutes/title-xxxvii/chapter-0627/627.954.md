# § 627.954 Rules.

The commission may establish and from time to time amend such rules relating to risk retention groups and purchasing groups as may be necessary or desirable to carry out the provisions of this part.

History: ss. 2, 5, ch. 87-282; s. 114, ch. 92-318; s. 1251, ch. 2003-261.
