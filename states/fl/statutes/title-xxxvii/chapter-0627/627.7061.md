# § 627.7061 Coverage inquiries.

Inquiries about coverage on a property insurance contract are not claim activity, unless an actual claim is filed by the policyholder which results in a company investigation of the claim.

History: s. 78, ch. 92-318; s. 23, ch. 2011-39.
