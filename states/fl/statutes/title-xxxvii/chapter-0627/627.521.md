# § 627.521 Disclosure statements.

Each industrial life insurance premium receipt book delivered in this state on or after January 1, 1983, shall contain the following disclosure statement: “You are entitled to a discount or refund from the company if you make your premium payment 13 weeks in advance.”

History: ss. 419, 809(2nd), ch. 82-243; s. 79, ch. 82-386; s. 114, ch. 92-318.
