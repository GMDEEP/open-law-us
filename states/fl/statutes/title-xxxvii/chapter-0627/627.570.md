# § 627.570 Premium rates.

A life insurer may issue insurance policies under the provisions of this part at premium rates less than the usual rates or premiums for individual insurance policies.

History: s. 543, ch. 59-205; s. 3, ch. 76-168; s. 1, ch. 77-457; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 443, 448, 809(2nd), ch. 82-243; s. 79, ch. 82-386; s. 114, ch. 92-318.
