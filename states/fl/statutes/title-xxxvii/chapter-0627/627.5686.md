# § 627.5686 Waiver of premium for disabled insured.

A waiver of premium for any insured who is totally disabled for a period of at least 6 months shall be made available to the policyholder as a part of the application for any group life insurance policy.

History: ss. 441, 809(2nd), ch. 82-243; s. 79, ch. 82-386; s. 114, ch. 92-318.
