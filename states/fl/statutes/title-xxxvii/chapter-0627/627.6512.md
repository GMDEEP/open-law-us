# § 627.6512 Exemption of certain group health insurance policies.

Sections 627.6561, 627.65615, 627.65625, and 627.6571 do not apply to any group insurance policy in relation to its provision of benefits described in s. 627.6513(1)-(14).

History: s. 7, ch. 97-179; s. 14, ch. 2016-194.
