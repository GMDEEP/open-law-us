# § 627.798 Rulemaking authority.

The commission shall by rule adopt a form to be used to provide notice to a purchaser-mortgagor that the purchaser-mortgagor is not protected by the title policy of the mortgagee.

History: s. 15, ch. 2000-370; s. 1212, ch. 2003-261.
