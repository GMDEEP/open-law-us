# § 627.6551 Teacher and student groups.

A group of teachers or students of an institution of learning may be insured under a policy issued by an insurer authorized under chapter 624 or a health maintenance organization authorized under chapter 641 to a school, district school systems, college, university, or other institution of learning. Any policy or contract issued may insure the spouse, dependent children, parents, or siblings of the insured student or teacher.

History: ss. 6, 7, ch. 89-190; s. 114, ch. 92-318.
