# § 627.661 School accident insurance claims; policy service.

An insurer issuing a school accident policy referred to in s. 627.659(3) shall maintain an office or offices in this state for the processing and payment of claims, and to render service to insureds, or shall appoint a duly licensed adjuster or resident agent for that purpose; except that processing and payment of claims and service may be performed at the insurer’s home office.
