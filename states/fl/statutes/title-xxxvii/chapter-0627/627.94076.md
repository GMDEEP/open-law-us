# § 627.94076 Time limit on certain defenses.

Notwithstanding the provisions of s. 627.607, each long-term care insurance policy shall provide that the policy shall be incontestable after it has been in force during the lifetime of the insured for a period of 2 years after its date of issue except for nonpayment of premiums.

History: s. 6, ch. 2006-254.
