# § 627.946 Countersignatures not required.

A policy of insurance issued to a risk retention group or any member of that group shall not be required to be countersigned by a resident agent notwithstanding any other provision of the Florida Insurance Code to the contrary.

History: ss. 2, 5, ch. 87-282; s. 114, ch. 92-318.
