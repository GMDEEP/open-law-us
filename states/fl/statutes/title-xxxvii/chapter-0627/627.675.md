# § 627.675 Mandated coverages inapplicable to Medicare supplement policies unless specifically made applicable.

No coverage which is required by a law of this state enacted on or after the effective date of this act to be included in group, individual, blanket, or franchise disability policies need be included in any Medicare supplement policy unless inclusion thereof is specifically made applicable to Medicare supplement policies by the terms of such law.
