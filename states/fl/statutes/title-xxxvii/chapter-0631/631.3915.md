# § 631.3915 Actions for damages.

The department, in its capacity as administrator, receiver, or similar capacity, may pursue any actions for damages or other recoveries on behalf of the insurer’s estate and the insurer’s policyholders, creditors, and other claimants.

History: s. 14, ch. 2002-25.
