# § 641.412 Fees.

(1) Every prepaid health clinic shall pay to the office the following fees:

(a) For filing a copy of its application for a certificate of authority or an amendment to such certificate, a nonrefundable fee in the amount of $150.

(b) For filing each annual report, a fee in the amount of $150.

(2) The fees charged under this section shall be distributed as follows:

(a) One-third of the total amount of fees shall be distributed to the Agency for Health Care Administration; and

(b) Two-thirds of the total amount of fees shall be distributed to the office.
