# § 641.56 Rulemaking authority.

The Agency for Health Care Administration has authority to adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement the provisions of this part conferring duties upon it.

History: ss. 24, 27, ch. 87-236; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 91, ch. 91-282; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 38, ch. 96-199; s. 215, ch. 98-200.
