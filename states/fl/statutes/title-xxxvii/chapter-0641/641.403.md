# § 641.403 Rulemaking authority.

The commission may adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement the provisions of this part.

History: ss. 10, 11, ch. 84-313; s. 13, ch. 87-236; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 214, ch. 98-200; s. 1600, ch. 2003-261.
