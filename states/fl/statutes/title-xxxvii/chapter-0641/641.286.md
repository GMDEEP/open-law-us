# § 641.286 Levy upon deposit limited.

No judgment creditor or other claimant of a health maintenance organization shall have the right to levy upon any of the assets or securities held in this state as a deposit under s. 641.285.

History: ss. 18, 47, ch. 85-177; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
