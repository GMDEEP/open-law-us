# § 641.447 Appeal from order.

Any person, entity, or prepaid health clinic that is subject to an order of the office under s. 641.446 may obtain a review of the order by filing an appeal from the order in accordance with the provisions and procedures for appeal under s. 120.68.

History: ss. 10, 11, ch. 84-313; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 1617, ch. 2003-261.
