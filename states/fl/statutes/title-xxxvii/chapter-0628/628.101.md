# § 628.101 Amendment of certificate of incorporation; stock insurer.

A domestic stock insurer shall not amend its certificate of incorporation until a copy of the proposed amendment has been filed with and approved by the office. The office shall promptly examine any such proposed amendment and shall approve the same unless it finds that the proposed amendment does not comply with law.
