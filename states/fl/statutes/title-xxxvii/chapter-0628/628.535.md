# § 628.535 Authority to adopt rules.

The commission may adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement the provisions of this chapter.

History: s. 7, ch. 85-214; s. 1, ch. 86-286; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 206, ch. 98-200; s. 1293, ch. 2003-261.
