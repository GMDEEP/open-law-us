# § 628.731 Application of holding company statutes and regulations.

Each reorganized subsidiary insurance company shall be subject to the applicable laws and rules of this state relating to insurance holding company systems. A mutual insurance holding company shall not be subject to provisions of this chapter or rules adopted thereunder with respect to the writing of insurance or required capital or surplus. A mutual insurance holding company system shall be considered an insurance holding company system but shall not require separate approval under this chapter for an acquisition of controlling stock, ownership interest, assets, or control, or for a merger or consolidation, share exchange, organization, or reorganization of insurance companies, or other transaction with respect to any action approved pursuant to the provisions of this part.

History: s. 1, ch. 97-216.
