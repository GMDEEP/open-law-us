# § 651.014 Insurance business not authorized.

Nothing in the Florida Insurance Code or this chapter shall be deemed to authorize any provider of a continuing care facility to transact any insurance business other than that of continuing care insurance or otherwise to engage in any other type of insurance unless it is authorized under a certificate of authority issued by the office under the provisions of the Florida Insurance Code.

History: ss. 32, 52, ch. 85-321; s. 12, ch. 93-22; s. 1665, ch. 2003-261.
