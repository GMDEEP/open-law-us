# § 625.3262 State of Israel obligations.

An insurer may invest in bonds, notes, warrants, and other evidences of indebtedness which are direct obligations of the State of Israel or for which the full faith and credit of the State of Israel is pledged for the payment of principal and interest.

History: s. 1, ch. 78-114; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 122, 809(1st), ch. 82-243; ss. 187, 188, ch. 91-108; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
