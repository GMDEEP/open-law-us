# § 633.428 Florida State Fire College established.

There is established a state institution to be known as the Florida State Fire College, to be located at or near Ocala, Marion County. The institution shall be operated by the division.

History: s. 3, ch. 25097, 1949; ss. 18, 35, ch. 69-106; ss. 1, 8, ch. 75-151; s. 1404, ch. 2003-261; s. 67, ch. 2013-183.

Note. — Former s. 163.532; s. 633.43.
