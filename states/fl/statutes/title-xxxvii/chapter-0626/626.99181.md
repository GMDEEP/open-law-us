# § 626.99181 Viatical settlement broker’s compensation.

A viatical settlement broker shall disclose to a prospective viator the amount and method of calculating the broker’s compensation. The term “compensation” includes anything of value paid or given to a viatical settlement broker for the placement of a policy.

History: s. 2, ch. 99-212.
