# § 626.9926 Rate regulation not authorized.

Nothing in this act shall be construed to authorize the office or department to directly or indirectly regulate the amount paid as consideration for entry into a viatical settlement contract.

History: s. 17, ch. 96-336; s. 8, ch. 99-212; s. 1056, ch. 2003-261; s. 26, ch. 2005-237.
