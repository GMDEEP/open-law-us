# § 626.99291 Contestability of life insurance policies.

Notwithstanding s. 627.455, a life insurer may contest a life insurance policy if the policy was obtained by a stranger-originated life insurance practice, as defined in s. 626.9911.

History: s. 11, ch. 2017-178.
