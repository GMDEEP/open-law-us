# § 287.19 Transfer of funds.

All moneys designated for or appropriated to any agency for the use, operation, maintenance, repair, or replacement of any state-owned or leased motor vehicles or aircraft shall be transferred to the Department of Management Services as required by the department.

History: s. 22, ch. 69-106; s. 91, ch. 98-279.
