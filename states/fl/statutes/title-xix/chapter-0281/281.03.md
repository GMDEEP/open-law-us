# § 281.03 Incident reports and record retention.

The Department of Management Services shall make provision for the collection and retention of copies of reports relating to criminal activity or other safety-related and security-related incidents occurring on state-owned property for use in ongoing security planning and to fulfill its responsibilities under s. 281.02.
