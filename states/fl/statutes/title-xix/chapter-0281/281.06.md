# § 281.06 Contracts with counties, municipalities, or licensed private security agencies.

The Department of Management Services may contract with any county, municipality, or licensed private security agency to provide and maintain the security of state-owned property, and the safety and security of occupants and visitors thereof, pursuant to ss. 281.02-281.08 upon such terms as the department may deem to be in the best interest of the state.
