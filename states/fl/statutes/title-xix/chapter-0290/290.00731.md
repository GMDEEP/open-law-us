# § 290.00731 Enterprise zone designation for Citrus County.

Citrus County may apply to the department for designation of one enterprise zone for an area within Citrus County. Notwithstanding s. 290.0065 limiting the total number of enterprise zones designated and the number of enterprise zones within a population category, the department may designate one enterprise zone under this section. The department shall establish the initial effective date of the enterprise zone designated under this section.

History: s. 19, ch. 2012-32; s. 51, ch. 2014-17.
