# § 280.19 Rules.

The Chief Financial Officer shall adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to administer the provisions of this chapter.

History: s. 3, ch. 81-285; s. 55, ch. 98-200; s. 307, ch. 2003-261.
