# § 617.1702 Application to qualified foreign corporations.

A foreign corporation authorized to conduct its affairs in this state on July 1, 1991, is subject to this chapter but is not required to obtain a new certificate of authority to conduct its affairs under this chapter.

History: s. 110, ch. 90-179; s. 149, ch. 2026-168.
