# § 617.0206 Bylaws.

The initial bylaws of a corporation shall be adopted by its board of directors unless that power is reserved to the members by the articles of incorporation. The power to alter, amend, or repeal the bylaws or adopt new bylaws is vested in the board of directors unless otherwise provided in the articles of incorporation or the bylaws. The bylaws may contain any provision for the regulation and management of the affairs of the corporation not inconsistent with law or the articles of incorporation.

History: s. 21, ch. 90-179; s. 14, ch. 2026-168.
