# § 617.0204 Liability for preincorporation transactions.

All persons purporting to act as or on behalf of a corporation, knowing that there was no incorporation under this chapter, are jointly and severally liable for all liabilities created while so acting.

History: s. 19, ch. 90-179; s. 13, ch. 2026-168.
