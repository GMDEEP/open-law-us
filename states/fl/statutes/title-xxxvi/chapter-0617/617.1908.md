# § 617.1908 Applicability of Florida Business Corporation Act.

Except as made applicable by specific reference in any other section of this chapter, part I of chapter 607, the Florida Business Corporation Act, does not apply to any nonprofit corporations.

History: s. 115, ch. 90-179; s. 76, ch. 93-281; s. 2, ch. 94-165; s. 49, ch. 2014-209; s. 156, ch. 2026-168.
