# § 607.1005 Amendment before issuance of shares.

If a corporation has not yet issued shares, its board of directors, or a majority of its incorporators if it has no board of directors, may adopt one or more amendments to the corporation’s articles of incorporation.

History: s. 101, ch. 89-154; s. 123, ch. 2019-90.
