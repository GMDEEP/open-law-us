# § 607.1711 Application to foreign and interstate commerce.

The provisions of this chapter apply to commerce with foreign nations and among the several states only insofar as the same may be permitted under the Constitution and laws of the United States.

History: s. 159, ch. 89-154; s. 228, ch. 2019-90.
