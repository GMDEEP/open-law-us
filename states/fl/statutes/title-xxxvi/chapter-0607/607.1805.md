# § 607.1805 Procedures for conversion to professional service corporation.

A corporation that is organized for profit under the laws of this state and that is engaged solely in carrying out the professional services provided by a corporation organized under chapter 621 may change its corporate nature to that of a professional service corporation if it complies with chapter 621.

History: s. 161, ch. 89-154.
