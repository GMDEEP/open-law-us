# § 610.116 Compliance.

If a certificateholder is found by a court of competent jurisdiction not to be in compliance with the requirements of this chapter, the certificateholder shall have a reasonable period of time, as specified by the court, to cure such noncompliance.

History: s. 7, ch. 2007-29.
