# § 620.1112 Business transactions of partner with partnership.

A partner may lend money to and transact other business with the limited partnership and, subject to s. 620.1408 and any other applicable provisions of this act, a partner has the same rights and obligations with respect to the loan or other transaction as a person that is not a partner.

History: s. 17, ch. 2005-267.
