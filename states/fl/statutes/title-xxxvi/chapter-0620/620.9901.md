# § 620.9901 Applicability.

Effective January 1, 1998, the Revised Uniform Partnership Act of 1995 governs all partnerships.

History: s. 14, ch. 95-242; s. 34, ch. 99-285; s. 43, ch. 2001-63; s. 115, ch. 2005-2.

Note. — Former s. 620.90.
