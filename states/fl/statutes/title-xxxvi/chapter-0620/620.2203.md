# § 620.2203 Relation to Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act.

This act modifies, limits, or supersedes the federal Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act, 15 U.S.C. ss. 7001 et seq., but this act does not modify, limit, or supersede s. 101(c) of that act, 15 U.S.C. s. 7001(c), or authorize electronic delivery of any of the notices described in s. 103(b) of that act, 15 U.S.C. s. 7001(b), except to the extent permitted pursuant to ss. 15.16, 116.34, and 668.50 of such act.

History: s. 17, ch. 2005-267.
