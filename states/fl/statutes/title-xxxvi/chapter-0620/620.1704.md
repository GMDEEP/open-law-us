# § 620.1704 Power of estate of deceased partner.

If a partner dies, the deceased partner’s personal representative or other legal representative may exercise the rights of a transferee as provided in s. 620.1702 and, for the purposes of settling the estate, may exercise the rights of a current limited partner under s. 620.1304.

History: s. 17, ch. 2005-267.
