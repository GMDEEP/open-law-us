# § 616.254 Authority of Florida State Fair Authority to sue and be a party to suits.

The authority may sue and be sued, plead and be impleaded, and complain and defend in all courts of law and equity with respect to its contractual rights and obligations and its responsibility to carry out its proper purposes and functions.

History: s. 4, ch. 74-322; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 25, 26, ch. 83-239; ss. 29, 44, ch. 93-168.
