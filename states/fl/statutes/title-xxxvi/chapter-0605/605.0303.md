# § 605.0303 Statement of denial.

A person who is named in a filed statement of authority granting that person authority may deliver to the department for filing a statement of denial signed by that person which:

(1) Provides the name of the limited liability company and the caption of the statement of authority to which the statement of denial pertains; and

(2) Denies the grant of authority.

History: s. 2, ch. 2013-180.
