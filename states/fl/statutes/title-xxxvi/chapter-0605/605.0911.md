# § 605.0911 Withdrawal deemed on conversion to domestic filing entity.

A registered foreign limited liability company authorized to transact business in this state that converts to a domestic limited liability company or to another domestic entity that is organized, incorporated, registered or otherwise formed through the delivery of a record to the department for filing is deemed to have withdrawn its certificate of authority on the effective date of the conversion.

History: s. 2, ch. 2013-180; s. 264, ch. 2019-90.
