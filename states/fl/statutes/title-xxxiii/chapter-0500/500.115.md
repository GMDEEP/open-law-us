# § 500.115 Advertisement of food deemed false.

An advertisement of a food is deemed to be false if it is false or misleading in any particular.

History: s. 19, ch. 19656, 1939; CGL 1940 Supp. 4151(682); ss. 19, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 424, ch. 77-147; s. 13, ch. 82-225.

Note. — Former s. 500.19.
