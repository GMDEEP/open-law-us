# § 516.33 Public disclosures.

All findings of facts and orders filed with the commission or office shall be a public record.

History: s. 12, ch. 73-192; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 16, 17, ch. 88-342; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 581, ch. 2003-261.
