# § 516.001 Short title.

This chapter shall hereafter be known, referred to, and cited as the “Florida Consumer Finance Act.”

History: s. 1, ch. 73-192; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 16, 17, ch. 88-342; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
