# § 542.20 Exemptions.

Any activity or conduct exempt under Florida statutory or common law or exempt from the provisions of the antitrust laws of the United States is exempt from the provisions of this part.

History: s. 1, ch. 80-28; s. 6, ch. 2025-213.
