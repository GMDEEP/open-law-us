# § 531.48 Declarations of unit price on random packages.

In addition to the declarations required by s. 531.47, any package being one of a lot containing random weights of the same commodity must bear on the outside of the package a plain and conspicuous declaration of the price per single unit of weight and the total retail price of the package, as defined by department rule.

History: s. 1, ch. 72-101; s. 57, ch. 2025-22.
