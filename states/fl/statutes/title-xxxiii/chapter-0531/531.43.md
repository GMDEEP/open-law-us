# § 531.43 Misrepresentation of quantity.

No person shall sell or offer or expose for sale less than the quantity he or she represents, nor take any more than the quantity he or she represents, when, as buyer, the person furnishes the weight or measure by means of which the quantity is determined.

History: s. 1, ch. 72-101; s. 730, ch. 97-103.
