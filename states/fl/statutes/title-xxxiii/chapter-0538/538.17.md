# § 538.17 Local regulation of secondhand dealers.

Nothing in this chapter shall preclude political subdivisions of the state and municipalities from enacting laws more restrictive than the provisions of this chapter.

History: s. 2, ch. 89-533; s. 1, ch. 90-192; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
