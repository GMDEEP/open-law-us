# § 538.33 Tendering payment.

Any payment by the mail-in secondhand precious metals dealer resulting from the sale, purchase, consignment, or trade of precious metals must be made to the person or entity by check to a lawful bank account or via a money services business licensed under part II of chapter 560.

History: s. 2, ch. 2009-158; s. 3, ch. 2009-162.
