# § 517.122 Arbitration.

Any agreement to provide services that are covered by this chapter, entered into after October 1, 1996, by a person required to register under this chapter, for arbitration of disputes arising under the agreement may provide to an aggrieved party the option of having arbitration before and pursuant to the rules of the American Arbitration Association or other independent nonindustry arbitration forum as well as any industry forum.

History: ss. 9, 15, ch. 86-85; ss. 8, 14, 15, ch. 90-362; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 6, ch. 96-338.
