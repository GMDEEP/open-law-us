# § 494.0039 Principal place of business requirements.

Each mortgage broker licensee shall maintain and transact business from a principal place of business.

History: ss. 26, 50, ch. 91-245; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 545, ch. 97-103; s. 15, ch. 99-213; s. 11, ch. 2006-213; s. 35, ch. 2009-241.
