# § 494.0077 Other products and services.

This part does not prohibit a mortgage lender from offering, for a fee or commission, products and services in addition to those offered in conjunction with making a mortgage loan.

History: ss. 49, 50, ch. 91-245; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 61, ch. 2009-241.
