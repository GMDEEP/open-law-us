# § 494.0022 Applicability of act.

Failure to comply with the provisions of this chapter does not affect the validity or enforceability of any mortgage loan; and no person acquiring a mortgage loan, as mortgagee or assignee, is required to ascertain whether or not the provisions of this chapter have been complied with.

History: ss. 13, 50, ch. 91-245; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
