# § 501.31 Legislative purpose.

The purpose of this part is to regulate the use of aftermarket crash parts by requiring disclosure when any use is proposed in an insurance estimate of a nonoriginal equipment manufacturer aftermarket crash part.

History: s. 2, ch. 89-241.
