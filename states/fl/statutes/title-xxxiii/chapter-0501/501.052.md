# § 501.052 Home solicitation sale; enforcement authority; injunctive relief.

The Attorney General or state attorney may institute proceedings to enjoin any person found to be violating the provisions of ss. 501.021-501.055.

History: s. 2, ch. 77-350; s. 12, ch. 97-250.
