# § 560.213 Payment instrument information.

Each payment instrument sold or issued by a licensee, directly or through an authorized vendor, must bear the name of the licensee, and any other information as may be required by rule, clearly imprinted thereon.

History: s. 2, ch. 94-238; s. 2, ch. 94-354; s. 38, ch. 2008-177.
