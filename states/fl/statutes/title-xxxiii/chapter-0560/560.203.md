# § 560.203 Exemptions from licensure.

Authorized vendors of a licensee acting within the scope of authority conferred by the licensee are exempt from licensure but are otherwise subject to the provisions of this chapter.

History: s. 2, ch. 94-238; s. 2, ch. 94-354; s. 29, ch. 2008-177.
