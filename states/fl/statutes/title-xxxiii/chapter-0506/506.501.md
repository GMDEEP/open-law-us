# § 506.501 Carts, Cases, Baskets, Boxes, and Containers Act; short title.

Sections 506.501-506.519 shall be known and may be cited as the “Carts, Cases, Baskets, Boxes, and Containers Act.”

History: s. 1, ch. 83-262; s. 1, ch. 87-80.
