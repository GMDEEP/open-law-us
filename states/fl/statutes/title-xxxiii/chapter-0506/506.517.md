# § 506.517 Deposits.

The requiring, taking, or accepting of any deposit upon delivery of any shopping cart, laundry cart, dairy case, egg basket, poultry box, or bakery container shall not be deemed a sale thereof, optional or otherwise.

History: s. 1, ch. 83-262; s. 9, ch. 87-80.
