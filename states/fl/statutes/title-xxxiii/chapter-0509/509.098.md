# § 509.098 Prohibition of hourly rates.

(1) An operator of a public lodging establishment may not offer an hourly rate for an accommodation.

(2) This section does not apply to an hourly rate charged by an operator of a public lodging establishment as a late checkout fee.

History: s. 5, ch. 2022-222.
