# § 509.416 Judgment for defendant when goods are not retained by or redelivered to the defendant.

If the property has not been retained by, or redelivered to, the defendant and the defendant prevails, judgment shall be entered against the plaintiff for possession of the property. Such judgment may include reasonable attorney’s fees and costs. The remedies provided in s. 509.415 and this section do not preclude any other remedies available under the laws of this state.
