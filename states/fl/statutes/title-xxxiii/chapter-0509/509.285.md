# § 509.285 Enforcement; city and county officers to assist.

Any state or county attorney, sheriff, police officer, and any other appropriate municipal and county official shall, upon request, assist the division or any of its agents in the enforcement of this chapter.

History: ss. 31, 52, ch. 90-339; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
