# § 559.934 Deceptive and unfair trade practice.

Acts, conduct, practices, omissions, failings, misrepresentations, or nondisclosures which constitute a violation of this part also constitute a deceptive and unfair trade practice for the purpose of s. 501.201 and administrative rules promulgated thereunder.

History: s. 1, ch. 93-107; s. 5, ch. 95-314.

Note. — Former s. 559.927(11).
