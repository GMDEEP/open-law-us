# § 548.033 Local prohibiting ordinance.

A permit may not be issued for a match to be held within any political subdivision of this state that has adopted a local ordinance or resolution prohibiting such matches.

History: ss. 2, 4, ch. 84-246; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
