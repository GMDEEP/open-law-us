# § 548.042 Participation under fictitious name.

A person may not participate under a fictitious or assumed name in any match unless she or he has registered the name with the commission.

History: ss. 2, 4, ch. 84-246; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 765, ch. 97-103; s. 122, ch. 99-251.
