# § 548.032 Permit for program of matches.

In addition to a promoter’s license, a promoter must procure a permit for each program of matches before presenting that program. Each application for a permit shall specify the location and time of the program.

History: ss. 2, 4, ch. 84-246; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
