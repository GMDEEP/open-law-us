# § 548.025 License fees.

The commission shall set license fees as follows:

(1) Promoter, matchmaker — not to exceed $500.

(2) Any other license — not to exceed $100.

History: ss. 2, 4, ch. 84-246; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 120, ch. 99-251.
