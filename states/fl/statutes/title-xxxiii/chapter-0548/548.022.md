# § 548.022 Oral examinations.

The commission may require any applicant or any officer, director, employee, or stockholder of a corporate applicant, before taking action on her or his application, to appear before the commission for an oral examination, under oath, as to her or his qualifications.

History: ss. 2, 4, ch. 84-246; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 764, ch. 97-103.
