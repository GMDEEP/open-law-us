# § 548.079 Misdemeanors.

Unless otherwise specified, any person convicted of any offense set forth in this chapter is guilty of a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

History: ss. 2, 4, ch. 84-246; s. 143, ch. 91-224; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
