# § 518.14 Scope of ss. 518.10-518.13.

The provisions of ss. 518.10-518.13 shall govern fiduciaries acting under wills, agreements, court orders, and other instruments now existing or hereafter made.

History: s. 9, ch. 28154, 1953.
