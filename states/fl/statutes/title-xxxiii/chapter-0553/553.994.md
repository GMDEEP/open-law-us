# § 553.994 Applicability.

Building energy-efficiency rating systems apply to all public, commercial, and residential buildings in the state.

History: s. 12, ch. 93-249; s. 4, ch. 94-284; s. 42, ch. 98-250; s. 28, ch. 2013-193.
