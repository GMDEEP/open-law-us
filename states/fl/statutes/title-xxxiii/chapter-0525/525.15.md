# § 525.15 Inspectors not to be interested in sales.

It is unlawful for any inspector to be interested, directly or indirectly, in the manufacturing or sale of any petroleum fuel regulated by this chapter.

History: s. 15, ch. 7905, 1919; CGL 3971; s. 77, ch. 92-291.
