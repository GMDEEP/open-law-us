# § 550.0745 Summer jai alai permit periods of operation.

A permitholder issued a permit under former subsection (1) of this section, Florida Statutes 2020, for the operation of a jai alai fronton during the summer season may conduct pari-mutuel wagering throughout the year.

History: s. 14, ch. 92-348; s. 9, ch. 2021-271.
