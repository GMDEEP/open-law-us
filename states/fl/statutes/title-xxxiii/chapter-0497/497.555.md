# § 497.555 Required rules.

Rules shall be adopted establishing minimum standards for access to all cemeteries by licensed monument establishments for the purpose of delivering and installing markers and monuments. In all cases, cemeteries and monument establishments shall comply with these minimum standards.

History: s. 123, ch. 2004-301; s. 45, ch. 2005-155.
