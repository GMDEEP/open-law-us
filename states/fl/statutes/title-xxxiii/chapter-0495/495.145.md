# § 495.145 Forum for actions regarding registration.

An action seeking cancellation of a registration of a mark registered under this chapter may be brought in any court of competent jurisdiction in this state. Service of process on a nonresident registrant may be made in accordance with ss. 48.161 and 48.181. The department may not be made a party to cancellation proceedings.

History: s. 16, ch. 2006-191; s. 18, ch. 2022-190.
