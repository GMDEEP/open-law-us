# § 540.02 Duty of state attorneys.

The state attorneys and the Department of Legal Affairs shall enforce the provisions of s. 540.01 by appropriate actions in courts of competent jurisdiction.

History: s. 3, ch. 6945, 1915; RGS 2518; CGL 3940; ss. 11, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 26, ch. 73-334.
