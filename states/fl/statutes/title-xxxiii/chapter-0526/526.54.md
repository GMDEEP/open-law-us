# § 526.54 Certified copy of analysis as evidence.

A certified copy of the analysis made by the department shall be admitted as prima facie evidence in any court proceeding involving the inspection, analysis, standards or specifications of brake fluid as defined and covered by this part.

History: s. 1, ch. 61-390.
