# § 526.03 Imitating trade names or equipment under which liquid fuel is marketed; prohibition.

It is unlawful for any person to disguise or camouflage his or her own equipment, by imitating the design, symbol, trade name, or the equipment under which recognized brands of liquid fuels, lubricating oils, and similar products, are generally marketed.

History: s. 3, ch. 16083, 1933; CGL 1936 Supp. 7315(4); s. 715, ch. 97-103.
