# § 502.015 General Inspection Trust Fund.

Any moneys collected by the department pursuant to this chapter shall be deposited in the General Inspection Trust Fund and used solely for the programs in this chapter.

History: s. 4, ch. 94-92; s. 28, ch. 2006-26; s. 15, ch. 2008-5.
