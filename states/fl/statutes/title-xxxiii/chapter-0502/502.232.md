# § 502.232 Local regulations superseded.

All special or local acts, general laws of limited application, county ordinances or resolutions, municipal ordinances or resolutions, and municipal charter provisions that authorize the regulation of milk or milk products, or frozen desserts for wholesale, are superseded by this chapter and the rules adopted pursuant to this chapter.

History: s. 2, ch. 74-370; s. 5, ch. 76-235; s. 5, ch. 93-68; ss. 13, 15, ch. 94-92; s. 28, ch. 2011-206.
