# § 331.325 Environmental permits.

Space Florida shall obtain required environmental permits in accordance with federal and state law and shall comply with the provisions of chapter 380.

History: ss. 29, 61, ch. 89-300; ss. 7, 8, ch. 90-192; s. 24, ch. 2006-60.
