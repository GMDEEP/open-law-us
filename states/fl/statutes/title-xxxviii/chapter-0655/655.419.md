# § 655.419 Effect.

The provisions of ss. 655.41-655.419 relating to merger, consolidation, conversion, or acquisition of assets of any financial institution are cumulative with all other provisions of the financial institutions codes and do not modify, limit, or repeal any other provisions except as expressly provided in the codes or as stated in an emergency order issued by the office pursuant to s. 655.4185.
