# § 655.934 Effect of lessee’s death or incapacity.

If a lessor without knowledge of the death or an order determining the incapacity of the lessee deals with the lessee’s agent in accordance with a written power of attorney or a durable power of attorney signed by such lessee, the transaction binds the lessee’s estate and the lessee.

History: s. 64, ch. 92-303; s. 1, ch. 2010-132.
