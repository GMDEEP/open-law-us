# § 662.135 Service as court-appointed fiduciary; bond requirement.

A licensed family trust company is not required to provide or otherwise post a bond or other surety to serve as a court-appointed fiduciary in a proceeding brought or conducted in this state.

History: s. 26, ch. 2014-97.
