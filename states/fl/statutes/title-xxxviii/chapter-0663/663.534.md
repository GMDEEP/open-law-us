# § 663.534 Events that require notice to be provided to the office.

A qualified limited service affiliate must report to the office, within 15 days of its knowledge of the occurrence, any changes to the information previously relied upon by the office when qualifying or renewing a qualification under this part.

History: s. 45, ch. 2017-83.
