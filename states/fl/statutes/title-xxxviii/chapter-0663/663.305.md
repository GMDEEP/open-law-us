# § 663.305 Investigation by the office.

Upon the filing of an application, the office shall make an investigation of such matters as it may deem appropriate, including the character, reputation, financial standing, business experience, and business qualifications of the proposed officers and directors.

History: ss. 1, 5, ch. 86-82; s. 1, ch. 91-307; s. 1, ch. 92-303; s. 1848, ch. 2003-261.
