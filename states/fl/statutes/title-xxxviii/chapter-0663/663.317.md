# § 663.317 Foreign currency lending, borrowing, and deposits.

An international development bank may make loans and other extensions of credit denominated in foreign currencies and may borrow funds and accept deposits denominated in foreign currencies.

History: ss. 1, 5, ch. 86-82; s. 8, ch. 89-110; s. 1, ch. 91-307; s. 1, ch. 92-303.
