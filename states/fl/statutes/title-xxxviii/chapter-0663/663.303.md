# § 663.303 Creation of an international development bank.

When authorized by the office as provided herein, a corporation may be formed under the laws of this state for the purpose of becoming an international development bank and engaging in activities authorized by this part.

History: ss. 1, 5, ch. 86-82; s. 1, ch. 91-307; s. 1, ch. 92-303; s. 1846, ch. 2003-261.
