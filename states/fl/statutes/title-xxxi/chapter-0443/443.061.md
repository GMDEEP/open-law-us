# § 443.061 Vested rights not created.

A right granted under this chapter is subject to amendment or repeal and does not create a vested right in any person.

History: s. 20, ch. 18402, 1937; CGL 1940 Supp. 4151(508); ss. 1, 8, 9, ch. 80-95; s. 21, ch. 2003-36.

Note. — Former s. 443.21.
