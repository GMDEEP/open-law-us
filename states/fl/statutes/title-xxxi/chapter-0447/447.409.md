# § 447.409 Records.

All records that are relevant to, or have a bearing upon, any issue or issues raised by the proceedings conducted by the special magistrate shall be made available to the special magistrate by a request in writing to any of the parties to the impasse proceedings. Notice of such request must be furnished to all parties. Any such records that are made available to the special magistrate must also be made available to any other party to the impasse proceedings, upon written request.
