# § 447.609 Representation in proceedings.

Any full-time employee or officer of any public employer or employee organization may represent his or her employer or any public employee in a bargaining unit in any proceeding authorized in this part, excluding the representation of any person or public employer in a court of law by a person who is not a licensed attorney.

History: s. 24, ch. 77-343; s. 163, ch. 97-103; s. 28, ch. 2026-58.
