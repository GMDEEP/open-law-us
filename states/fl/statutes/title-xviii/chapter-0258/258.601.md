# § 258.601 Enforcement of prohibited activities.

Prohibited activities under this chapter shall be enforced by the Department of Environmental Protection and the Division of Law Enforcement of the Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission and its officers.

History: s. 6, ch. 2012-88.
