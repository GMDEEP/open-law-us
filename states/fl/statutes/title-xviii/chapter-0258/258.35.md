# § 258.35 Short title; ss. 258.35-258.394 and 258.40-258.46.

Sections 258.35-258.394 and 258.40-258.46 shall be known and may be cited as the “Florida Aquatic Preserve Act of 1975.”

History: s. 1, ch. 75-172.
