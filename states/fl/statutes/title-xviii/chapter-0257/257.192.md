# § 257.192 Program grants.

The Division of Library and Information Services is authorized to accept and administer appropriations for library program grants and to make such grants in accordance with the Florida long-range plan for library services.

History: s. 5, ch. 73-138; s. 24, ch. 86-163; s. 5, ch. 2005-207.
