# § 274.08 Penalty.

Any person who violates any provision of this act or any rule prescribed pursuant to its authority shall be guilty of a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

History: s. 8, ch. 59-163; s. 158, ch. 71-136.
