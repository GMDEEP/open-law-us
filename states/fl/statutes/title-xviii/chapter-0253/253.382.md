# § 253.382 Oyster beds, minerals, and oils reserved to state.

The state saves, reserves and excepts all natural oyster beds upon and all minerals and oils in or under the submerged lands until the same shall be filled in and improved by the riparian owner.

History: s. 4, ch. 8537, 1921; CGL 1777.

Note. — Former s. 271.04.
