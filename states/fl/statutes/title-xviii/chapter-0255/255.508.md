# § 255.508 Participation in pool.

To participate in the pool, an agency head shall submit a request to the Department of Management Services and to the division pursuant to rules adopted by the Department of Management Services pursuant to this act.

History: s. 10, ch. 85-349; s. 28, ch. 98-279.
