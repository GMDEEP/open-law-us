# § 256.02 Certain officers to provide flag.

The officer charged with the maintenance or upkeep of the buildings mentioned in s. 256.01 shall provide suitable flags and cause them to be displayed, the expense to be borne out of the funds provided for the upkeep and maintenance of said buildings.

History: s. 2, ch. 7369, 1917; RGS 1207; CGL 1684.
