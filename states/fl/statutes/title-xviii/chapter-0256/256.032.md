# § 256.032 Display of state flag at public schools.

The state flag shall be displayed at a suitable place and in the appropriate manner on the grounds of each elementary and secondary public school. The school boards shall furnish flags whenever necessary.

History: s. 1, ch. 65-200; s. 1, ch. 69-300.
