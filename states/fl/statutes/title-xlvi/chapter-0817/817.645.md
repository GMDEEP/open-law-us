# § 817.645 Alteration of credit card invoice; penalties.

Whoever, with intent to defraud any person, falsely alters any invoice for money, goods, services, or anything else of value obtained by use of a credit card after it has been signed by the cardholder or a person authorized by him or her violates this section and is subject to the penalties set forth in s. 817.67(1).

History: s. 1, ch. 72-127; s. 1273, ch. 97-102.
