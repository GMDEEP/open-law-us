# § 817.68 Part II not exclusive.

This part shall not be construed to preclude the applicability of any other provision of the criminal law of this state which presently applies or may in the future apply to any transaction which violates this part, unless such provision is inconsistent with the terms of this part.

History: s. 11, ch. 67-340.
