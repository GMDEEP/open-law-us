# § 817.031 Making false statements; venue of prosecution.

Prosecutions under s. 817.03 may be begun in the county where the statement was written, or purports to have been written.

History: s. 2, ch. 5134, 1903; GS 3323; RGS 5161; CGL 7264.

Note. — Former s. 817.04.
