# § 817.47 Insurance advertising exempt.

Nothing in ss. 817.40, 817.41, 817.43-817.45, and 817.47 shall be deemed to apply to advertising in connection with sales of insurance which are regulated under the insurance laws of this state.

History: s. 9, ch. 59-301; s. 74, ch. 95-211.
