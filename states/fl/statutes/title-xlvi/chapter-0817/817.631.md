# § 817.631 Possession and transfer of credit-card-making equipment.

A person who receives, possesses, transfers, buys, sells, controls, or has custody of any credit-card-making equipment with intent that such equipment be used in the production of counterfeit credit cards violates this section and is subject to the penalties set forth in s. 817.67(2).

History: s. 6, ch. 85-43.
