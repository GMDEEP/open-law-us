# § 876.04 Allowing unlawful assembly in building prohibited.

No owner, agent, superintendent, janitor, caretaker, or occupant of any place, building, or room, shall willfully and knowingly permit therein any assemblage of persons prohibited by s. 876.03, and if such person after notification that the premises are so used permits such use to be continued he or she shall be guilty of a misdemeanor of the first degree, punishable as provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

History: s. 4, ch. 20216, 1941; s. 1139, ch. 71-136; s. 1413, ch. 97-102.
