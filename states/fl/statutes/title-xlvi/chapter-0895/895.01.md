# § 895.01 Short title.

Sections 895.01-895.06 shall be known as the “Florida RICO (Racketeer Influenced and Corrupt Organization) Act.”

History: s. 1, ch. 77-334; s. 2, ch. 79-218.

Note. — Former s. 943.46.
