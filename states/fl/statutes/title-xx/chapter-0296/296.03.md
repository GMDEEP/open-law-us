# § 296.03 Veterans’ Domiciliary Home of Florida.

The Veterans’ Domiciliary Home of Florida is for veterans who served in wartime service or peacetime service, as defined in s. 296.02, or the spouses or surviving spouses of such veterans, and is maintained for the use of those individuals who are not in need of hospitalization or nursing home care and who can attend to their personal needs, dress themselves, and attend a general dining facility, or who are in need of extended congregate care.

History: s. 8, ch. 89-168; s. 6, ch. 92-80; s. 2, ch. 98-16; s. 2, ch. 2024-250.
