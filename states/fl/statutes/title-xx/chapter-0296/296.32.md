# § 296.32 Purpose.

The purpose of this part is to provide for the establishment of basic standards for the operation of veterans’ nursing homes for eligible veterans and the spouses or surviving spouses of such veterans who are in need of such services.

History: s. 14, ch. 92-80; s. 4, ch. 2002-298; s. 4, ch. 2024-250.
