# § 705.1015 County or municipal code inspectors or code enforcement officers; duties.

Employees of a county or municipality whose duty it is to ensure code compliance or enforce codes and ordinances may be designated by the governing body of the county or the municipality to administer the provisions of this chapter which pertain to lost or abandoned property. Designation of such employees shall not provide the employees with the authority to bear arms or make arrests.

History: s. 16, ch. 89-268.
