# § 713.56 Liens for labor on and with machines, etc.

In favor of any person by herself or himself or others performing any labor upon or with any engine, machine, apparatus, fixture, implement, newspaper or printing material or other property, or doing work in any hotel; upon such engine, machine, material, apparatus, fixture, implement, newspaper or printing material, or other property, and upon the furniture, furnishings and belongings of said hotel.

History: s. 6, ch. 3747, 1887; RS 1730; s. 1, ch. 4583, 1897; GS 2196; RGS 3503; CGL 5364; s. 36, ch. 67-254; s. 821, ch. 97-102.

Note. — Former s. 85.07.
