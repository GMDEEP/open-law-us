# § 713.63 Liens for furnishing locomotives, machinery, etc.

In favor of any person who shall furnish any locomotive or stationary engine, water engine, windmill, car or other machine or parts of machine or instrument for any railroad, telegraph or telephone line, mill, distillery, or other manufactory; upon the articles so furnished.

History: s. 9, ch. 3747, 1887; RS 1737; GS 2203; RGS 3510; CGL 5371; s. 36, ch. 67-254.

Note. — Former s. 85.14.
