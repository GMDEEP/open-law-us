# § 695.015 Conveyances by law between governmental agencies, recording.

All laws which purport to convey title to real property from one governmental agency or political subdivision to another shall be recorded in the public records of the county or counties in which the property is located, and such laws shall contain a provision requiring such recording.

History: s. 1, ch. 70-103.
