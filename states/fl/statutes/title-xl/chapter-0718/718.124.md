# § 718.124 Limitation on actions by association.

The statute of limitations and statute of repose for any actions in law or equity which a condominium association or a cooperative association may have shall not begin to run until the unit owners have elected a majority of the members of the board of administration.

History: s. 9, ch. 77-222; s. 263, ch. 79-400; s. 13, ch. 2024-244.
