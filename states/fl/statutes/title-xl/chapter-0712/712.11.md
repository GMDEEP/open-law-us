# § 712.11 Covenant revitalization.

A property owners’ association not otherwise subject to chapter 720 may use the procedures set forth in ss. 720.403-720.407 to revive covenants that have lapsed under the terms of this chapter.

History: s. 1, ch. 2007-173; s. 5, ch. 2018-55.
