# § 717.111 Property of business associations held in course of dissolution.

All intangible property distributable in the course of a voluntary or involuntary dissolution of a business association which is not claimed by the apparent owner for more than 6 months after the date specified for final distribution is presumed abandoned.

History: s. 12, ch. 87-105; s. 12, ch. 2001-36; s. 49, ch. 2026-174.
