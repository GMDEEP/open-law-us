# § 703.17 Alteration of abstracts condemned by county for use of the public.

Any person or persons making any erasure, alteration, interlineation, or interpolation in any abstracts, copies, minutes, extracts, maps, or plats, or in any copies thereof filed in the clerk’s office under the provisions of ss. 703.09-703.11 and 703.13-703.15 shall be guilty of the crime of forgery.

History: s. 9, ch. 5414, 1905; RGS 5207; CGL 7325; s. 678, ch. 71-136.
