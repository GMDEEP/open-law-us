# § 711.512 Application of ss. 711.50-711.512.

Sections 711.50-711.512 apply to registrations of securities in beneficiary form made before, on, or after January 1, 1995, by decedents dying on or after January 1, 1995.

History: s. 3, ch. 94-216.
