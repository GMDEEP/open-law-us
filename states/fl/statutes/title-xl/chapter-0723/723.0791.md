# § 723.0791 Mobile home cooperative homeowners’ associations; elections.

The provisions of s. 719.106(1)(b) notwithstanding, the election of board members in a mobile home cooperative homeowners’ association may be carried out in the manner provided for in the bylaws of the association. A mobile home cooperative is a residential cooperative consisting of real property to which 10 or more mobile homes are located or are affixed.

History: s. 33, ch. 93-150.
