# § 719.127 Receivership notification.

Upon the appointment of a receiver by a court for any reason relating to a cooperative association, the court shall direct the receiver to provide to all unit owners written notice of his or her appointment as receiver. Such notice shall be mailed or delivered within 10 days after the appointment. Notice by mail to a unit owner shall be sent to the address used by the county property appraiser for notice to the unit owner.

History: s. 7, ch. 2008-202.
