# § 11.2422 Statutes repealed.

Every statute of a general and permanent nature enacted by the State or by the Territory of Florida at or prior to the 2025 regular legislative session, and every part of such statute, not included in Florida Statutes 2026, as adopted by s. 11.2421, as amended, or recognized and continued in force by reference therein or in ss. 11.2423 and 11.2424, as amended, is repealed.
