# § 11.9337 Filing of oath; issuance of commission.

The executed oath of a delegate or alternate delegate shall be filed with the Secretary of State. After the oath is filed, the Governor shall issue a commission to the delegate or alternate delegate.

History: s. 11, ch. 2014-52.
