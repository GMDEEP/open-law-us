# § 455.116 Regulation trust funds.

The following trust funds shall be placed in the department:

(1) Administrative Trust Fund.

(2) Alcoholic Beverage and Tobacco Trust Fund.

(3) Cigarette Tax Collection Trust Fund.

(4) Hotel and Restaurant Trust Fund.

(5) Division of Florida Condominiums, Timeshares, and Mobile Homes Trust Fund.

(6) Professional Regulation Trust Fund.
