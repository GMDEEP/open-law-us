# § 492.101 Purpose.

It is hereby declared to be the public policy of the state that, in order to safeguard the life, health, property, and public well-being of its citizens, any person practicing or offering to practice geology in this state shall meet the requirements of this chapter.

History: ss. 1, 19, ch. 87-403; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 194, ch. 94-218; s. 55, ch. 2000-356.
