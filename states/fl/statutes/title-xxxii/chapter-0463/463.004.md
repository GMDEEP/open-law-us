# § 463.004 Board headquarters.

The board shall maintain its official headquarters in the City of Tallahassee.

History: ss. 1, 6, ch. 79-194; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 20, 21, ch. 86-289; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
