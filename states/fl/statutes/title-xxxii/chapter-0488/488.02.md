# § 488.02 Rules and regulations.

The Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles has authority to adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement the provisions of this chapter.

History: s. 2, ch. 28142, 1953; ss. 24, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 2, 10, 11, ch. 84-150; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 159, ch. 98-200.
