# § 467.014 Financial responsibility.

A licensed midwife shall include in the informed consent plan presented to the parents the status of the midwife’s malpractice insurance, including the amount of malpractice insurance, if any.

History: ss. 10, 19, ch. 92-179; s. 79, ch. 2001-62.
