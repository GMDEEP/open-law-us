# § 466.02751 Establishment of practitioner profile for designation as a controlled substance prescribing practitioner.

The Department of Health shall establish a practitioner profile for dentists licensed under this chapter for a practitioner’s designation as a controlled substance prescribing practitioner as provided in s. 456.44.

History: s. 29, ch. 2011-141; s. 23, ch. 2016-224.
