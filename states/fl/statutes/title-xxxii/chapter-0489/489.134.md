# § 489.134 Authority of licensed job scope.

A licensee under this part need not have a license under part II to perform work within the scope of his or her license under this part.

History: s. 35, ch. 90-228; s. 11, ch. 91-201; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 488, ch. 97-103.
