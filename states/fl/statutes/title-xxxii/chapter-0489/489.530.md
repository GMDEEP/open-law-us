# § 489.530 Audible alarms.

Every audible alarm system installed by a licensed contractor shall have a device to automatically terminate the audible signal within 15 minutes of activation. A fire alarm system, whether installed voluntarily or as a requirement of an adopted code, which employs an audible fire signal is exempt as required by such code.

History: s. 18, ch. 96-298; s. 7, ch. 2006-154.
