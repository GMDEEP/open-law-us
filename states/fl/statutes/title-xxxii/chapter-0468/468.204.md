# § 468.204 Authority to adopt rules.

The board has authority to adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement the provisions of this part conferring duties upon it.

History: ss. 2, 13, ch. 84-4; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 133, ch. 98-200.
