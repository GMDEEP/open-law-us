# § 468.369 Payment or reimbursement by hospitals of costs of compliance with part.

A hospital shall not be required to pay for or reimburse any person for the costs of compliance with any of the requirements of this part, including costs of continuing education.

History: ss. 1, 3, ch. 84-252; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
