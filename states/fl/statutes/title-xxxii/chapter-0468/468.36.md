# § 468.36 Current address.

Every licensee shall file with the department the licensee’s current residence address as defined by board rule.

History: ss. 1, 3, ch. 84-252; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 293, ch. 97-103; s. 137, ch. 97-264.
