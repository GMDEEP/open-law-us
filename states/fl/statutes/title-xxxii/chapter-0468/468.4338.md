# § 468.4338 Reactivation; continuing education.

The council shall prescribe by rule continuing education requirements for reactivating a license. The continuing education requirements for reactivating a license may not exceed one renewal cycle of continuing education.

History: s. 8, ch. 96-291; s. 5, ch. 2012-61.
