# § 479.311 Jurisdiction; venue.

The county court shall have jurisdiction concurrent with the circuit court to consider claims filed by the department in amounts which are within their jurisdictional limitations. For the purposes of a claim filed by the department to recover its cost as provided in this section, venue shall be Leon County.

History: s. 43, ch. 2010-225.
