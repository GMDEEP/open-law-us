# § 475.255 Determination of agency or transactional brokerage relationship.

Without consideration of the related facts and circumstances, the mere payment or promise to pay compensation to a licensee does not determine whether an agency or transactional brokerage relationship exists between the licensee and a seller, landlord, buyer, or tenant.

History: s. 142, ch. 94-119.
