# § 475.001 Purpose.

The Legislature deems it necessary in the interest of the public welfare to regulate real estate brokers, sales associates, and schools in this state.

History: ss. 1, 42, ch. 79-239; ss. 2, 3, ch. 81-318; ss. 28, 30, ch. 88-20; s. 10, ch. 91-89; s. 4, ch. 91-429; s. 1, ch. 93-261; s. 13, ch. 2000-332; s. 21, ch. 2003-164.
