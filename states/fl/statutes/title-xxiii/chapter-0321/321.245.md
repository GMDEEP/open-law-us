# § 321.245 Disposition of certain funds in the Highway Safety Operating Trust Fund.

The director of the Florida Highway Patrol, after receiving recommendations from the commander of the auxiliary, is authorized to purchase uniforms and equipment for auxiliary law enforcement officers as defined in s. 321.24 from funds described in s. 324.0221(3). The amounts expended under this section shall not exceed $50,000 in any one fiscal year.

History: s. 2, ch. 95-202; s. 26, ch. 96-413; s. 3, ch. 2007-324.
