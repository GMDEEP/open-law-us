# § 320.08001 Electric vehicles; license tax.

The license tax for an electric vehicle or low-speed vehicle is the same as that prescribed in s. 320.08 for a vehicle that is not electrically powered.

History: s. 11, ch. 95-333; s. 3, ch. 99-163.
