# § 132.27 Chapter complete within itself.

This chapter constitutes full authority for the things herein authorized, and no proceedings, publications, notices, consents or approval shall be required for the doing of the things herein authorized except as are herein prescribed and required. This chapter shall be deemed complete within itself, except insofar as other laws are specifically made applicable, nor shall powers hereby granted be restricted or limited by any other law.

History: s. 27, ch. 15772, 1931; CGL 1936 Supp. 2383(27).
