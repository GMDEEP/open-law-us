# § 153.65 Trust funds; trustees.

The proceeds of all bonds or other obligations issued under this law and all revenues derived from the operation of any water system or sewer system for the payment of all or part of the cost of which any bonds or other obligations authorized by this law have been issued shall be and constitute trust funds, and shall be used and applied only in accordance with the proceedings authorizing the issuance of any revenue bonds, general obligation bonds or other obligations issued pursuant to this law, and the district may appoint trustees, within or without the state, under trust agreements or indentures to hold and administer the proceeds of any such bonds or other obligations or any such revenues.

History: s. 16, ch. 59-466.
