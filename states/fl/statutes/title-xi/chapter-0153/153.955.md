# § 153.955 Acquisition of privately owned water-wastewater utility.

If the applicant for financial assistance demonstrates, based on documentation acceptable to the department, that acquisition of a privately owned water-wastewater utility qualifies under s. 153.954, the department may authorize financial assistance for such acquisition.

History: s. 5, ch. 2001-229.
