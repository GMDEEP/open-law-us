# § 159.51 Powers of chapter supplemental.

The powers conferred by ss. 159.44-159.53 shall be in addition and supplementary to existing powers and statutes, and said sections shall not be construed as repealing any of the provisions of any other law, general or local.

History: s. 8, ch. 70-229.
