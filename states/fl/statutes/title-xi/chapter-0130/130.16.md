# § 130.16 Compensation of trustees.

The said trustees shall have such compensation for their services as follows: For receiving the first $10,000, 1.5 percent; for all over $10,000, 0.5 percent; for disbursements, the same as allowed for receiving, to be paid out of the county treasury.

History: s. 18, ch. 2088, 1877; RS 608; GS 804; RGS 1549; CGL 2327.
