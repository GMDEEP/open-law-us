# § 130.21 Time warrants in newly created counties; interest and date of maturity.

Such warrants shall bear interest at the rate of 6 percent per annum, payable annually, and shall mature on or before a date 5 years after the date of the issue of such warrants.

History: s. 2, ch. 8518, 1921; CGL 2351.
