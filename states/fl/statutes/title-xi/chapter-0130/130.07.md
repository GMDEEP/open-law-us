# § 130.07 Form of bonds.

The county commissioners may prescribe the form and the denominations of the bonds to be issued, and such bonds may be issued with or without interest coupons, as may be deemed expedient.

History: s. 7, ch. 2088, 1877; RS 599; GS 795; RGS 1540; CGL 2318.
