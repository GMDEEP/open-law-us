# § 129.021 County officer budget information.

Notwithstanding other provisions of law, the budgets of all county officers, as submitted to the board of county commissioners, must be in sufficient detail and contain such information as the board of county commissioners may require in furtherance of their powers and responsibilities provided in ss. 125.01(1)(q), (r), and (v), and (6) and 129.01(2)(c).

History: s. 2, ch. 78-132; s. 6, ch. 2011-144; s. 3, ch. 2023-306.
