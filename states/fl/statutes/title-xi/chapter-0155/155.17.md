# § 155.17 County hospitals; rules for physicians, nurses, etc.

When such hospital is established, the physician, nurses, attendants, the person sick therein and all persons approaching or coming within the limits of same, and all furniture and other articles used or brought there shall be subject to such rules and regulations as said board of trustees may prescribe.

History: s. 14, ch. 20905, 1941.
