# § 155.08 County hospitals; seal; evidence.

The board of trustees as herein constituted shall adopt a common seal, and certification under the seal of the board of hospital trustees, signed by the chair and attested by the secretary and treasurer, shall constitute sufficient evidence.

History: s. 5, ch. 20905, 1941; s. 877, ch. 95-147.
