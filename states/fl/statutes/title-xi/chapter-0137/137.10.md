# § 137.10 Provisions not applicable to surety companies.

The provisions of this chapter requiring two sureties and justification by surety shall not apply where such surety is a surety company authorized to do business in this state.

History: s. 1, ch. 5372, 1905; GS 830; RGS 1577; CGL 2425.
