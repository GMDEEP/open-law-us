# § 163.3243 Enforcement.

Any party or aggrieved or adversely affected person as defined in s. 163.3215(2) may file an action for injunctive relief in the circuit court where the local government is located to enforce the terms of a development agreement or to challenge compliance of the agreement with ss. 163.3220-163.3243.

History: s. 31, ch. 86-191; s. 27, ch. 2011-139.
