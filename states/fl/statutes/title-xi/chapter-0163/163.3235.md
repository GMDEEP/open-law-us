# § 163.3235 Periodic review of a development agreement.

A local government shall review land subject to a development agreement at least once every 12 months to determine if there has been demonstrated good faith compliance with the terms of the development agreement. If the local government finds, on the basis of substantial competent evidence, that there has been a failure to comply with the terms of the development agreement, the agreement may be revoked or modified by the local government.

History: s. 27, ch. 86-191; s. 12, ch. 92-129; s. 25, ch. 2011-139.
