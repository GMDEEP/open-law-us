# § 163.572 Expansion of area.

Upon a resolution adopted by the governing body of any adjoining county, municipality, or other political subdivision, the authority may, subject to the provisions of s. 163.567(1), by a majority vote of its membership, include such territory in its regional transportation area.

History: s. 8, ch. 71-373; s. 1, ch. 73-278.
