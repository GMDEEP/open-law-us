# § 161.131 Construction of ss. 161.011-161.212.

The provisions of ss. 161.011-161.212 shall be liberally construed by all concerned in a manner to best accomplish the beach and shore preservation purposes and programs.

History: s. 3, ch. 65-408; s. 6, ch. 86-138.
