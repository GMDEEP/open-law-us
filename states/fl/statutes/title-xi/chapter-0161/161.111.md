# § 161.111 Shore erosion emergency.

If a shore erosion emergency is declared by the Governor, the state, acting through the department, may spend whatever state funds are available to alleviate shore erosion.

History: s. 1, ch. 65-408; ss. 25, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 27, ch. 94-356; s. 10, ch. 2000-346.
