# § 222.09 Injunction to prevent sale.

The circuit courts have equity jurisdiction to enjoin the sale of all property, real and personal, that is exempt from forced sale.

History: s. 1, ch. 3246, 1881; RS 2006; GS 2528; RGS 3883; CGL 5790.
