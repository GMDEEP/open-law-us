# § 1006.695 Sexual predator and sexual offender notification; Florida College System institutions, state universities, and career centers.

Each Florida College System institution as defined in s. 1000.21, state university as defined in s. 1000.21, and career center as provided in s. 1001.44 shall inform students and employees at orientation and on its website of the existence of the Department of Law Enforcement sexual predator and sexual offender registry website and the toll-free telephone number that gives access to sexual predator and sexual offender public information pursuant to s. 943.043.

History: s. 4, ch. 2014-3.
