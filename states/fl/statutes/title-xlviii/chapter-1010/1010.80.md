# § 1010.80 Educational Media and Technology Trust Fund.

Chapter 99-25, Laws of Florida, re-created the Educational Media and Technology Trust Fund to record revenue and disbursements by the Department of Education for the cost of producing and disseminating educational materials and products as authorized in s. 1006.39.

History: s. 592, ch. 2002-387.
