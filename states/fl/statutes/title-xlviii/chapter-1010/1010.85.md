# § 1010.85 Phosphate Research Trust Fund.

Chapter 99-45, Laws of Florida, re-created the Phosphate Research Trust Fund to record the revenue and disbursements from tax on severance of phosphate rock as provided in s. 211.3103.

History: s. 597, ch. 2002-387.
