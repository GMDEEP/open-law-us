# § 1010.33 Financial and performance audits.

Each district school board and Florida College System institution board of trustees, and university board of trustees is authorized to have an audit of their accounts and records by an independent certified public accountant retained by them and paid from their public funds. These audits are in addition to those required by ss. 11.45 and 218.39.

History: s. 546, ch. 2002-387; s. 161, ch. 2011-5.
