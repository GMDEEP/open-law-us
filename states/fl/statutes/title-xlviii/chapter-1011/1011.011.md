# § 1011.011 Legislative capital outlay budget request.

The State Board of Education shall submit an integrated, comprehensive budget request for educational facilities construction and fixed capital outlay needs for school districts, Florida College System institutions, and, in conjunction with the Board of Governors, universities pursuant to this section and s. 1013.46 and applicable provisions of chapter 216.

History: s. 602, ch. 2002-387; s. 150, ch. 2007-217; s. 165, ch. 2011-5.
