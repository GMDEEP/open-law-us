# § 1011.66 Distribution of FEFP funds.

The distribution of FEFP funds shall be made in payments on or about the 10th and 26th of each month.

History: s. 658, ch. 2002-387; s. 10, ch. 2004-271; s. 27, ch. 2010-154.
