# § 1013.26 Department of Legal Affairs to represent university board in condemnation proceedings.

Any suits or actions brought by a university board of trustees to condemn property, as provided in s. 1013.25, shall be brought in the name of the university board of trustees, and the Department of Legal Affairs shall conduct the proceedings for, and act as the counsel of, the university board of trustees.

History: s. 820, ch. 2002-387.
