# § 1009.85 Participation in guaranteed student loan program.

The intent of this act is to authorize student loans when this state, through the Department of Education, has become an eligible lender under the provisions of the applicable federal laws providing for the guarantee of loans to students and the partial payment of interest on such loans by the United States Government.

History: s. 467, ch. 2002-387; s. 28, ch. 2013-35.
