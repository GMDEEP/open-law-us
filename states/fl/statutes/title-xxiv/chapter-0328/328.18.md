# § 328.18 Power to conduct investigations.

The Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles or any other law enforcement agency may make any investigation necessary to secure information required to carry out and enforce the provisions of this chapter.

History: s. 10, ch. 84-184; s. 62, ch. 95-333.
