# § 327.58 Jurisdiction.

The safety regulations included under this chapter shall apply to all vessels, except as specifically excluded, operating upon the waters of this state.

History: s. 1, ch. 65-361; s. 27, ch. 79-334; s. 10, ch. 81-100; s. 79, ch. 82-226.

Note. — Former s. 371.66.
