# § 327.71 Exemption.

The commission may, if it finds that federal law imposes less restrictive requirements than provided herein or if it determines that boating safety will not be adversely affected, issue temporary exemptions from any provision of this chapter or rules established hereunder, on such terms and conditions as it considers appropriate.

History: s. 11, ch. 81-100; s. 31, ch. 99-245.
