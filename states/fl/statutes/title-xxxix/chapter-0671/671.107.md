# § 671.107 Waiver or renunciation of claim or right after breach.

A claim or right arising out of an alleged breach can be discharged in whole or in part without consideration by agreement of the aggrieved party in a signed record.

History: s. 1, ch. 65-254; s. 7, ch. 2007-134; s. 35, ch. 2025-92.

Note. — s. 1-107, U.C.C.; supersedes ss. 675.28, 675.30.
