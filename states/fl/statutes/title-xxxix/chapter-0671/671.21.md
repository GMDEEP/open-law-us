# § 671.21 Presumptions.

Whenever this code creates a “presumption” with respect to a fact or provides that a fact is “presumed,” the trier of fact must find the existence of the fact presumed unless evidence is introduced which supports a finding of its nonexistence.

History: s. 16, ch. 2007-134.
