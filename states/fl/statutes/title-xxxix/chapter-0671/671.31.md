# § 671.31 Transition provisions as to priorities.

Except as otherwise provided in this part, chapter 65-254, Laws of Florida, as amended, shall apply to any questions of priority if the positions of the parties were fixed prior to the effective date of chapter 79-398, Laws of Florida.

History: s. 39, ch. 79-398; s. 6, ch. 90-278.

Note. — Former s. 680.11.
