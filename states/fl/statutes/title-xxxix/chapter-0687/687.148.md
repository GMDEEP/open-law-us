# § 687.148 Duties and powers of the commission and office.

(1) The office is responsible for the administration and enforcement of this act.

(2) The commission may adopt such rules as it may deem necessary in the administration of this act and not inconsistent therewith.

History: s. 9, ch. 91-87; s. 1878, ch. 2003-261.
