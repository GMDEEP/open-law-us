# § 682.05 Majority action by arbitrators.

If there is more than one arbitrator, the powers of an arbitrator must be exercised by a majority of the arbitrators, but all of the arbitrators shall conduct the hearing under s. 682.06(3).

History: s. 4, ch. 57-402; s. 12, ch. 67-254; s. 14, ch. 2013-232.

Note. — Former s. 57.14.
