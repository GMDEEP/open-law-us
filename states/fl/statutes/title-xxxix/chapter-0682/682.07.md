# § 682.07 Representation by attorney.

A party has the right to be represented by an attorney at any arbitration proceeding or hearing under this law.

History: s. 6, ch. 57-402; s. 12, ch. 67-254; s. 17, ch. 2013-232.

Note. — Former s. 57.16.
