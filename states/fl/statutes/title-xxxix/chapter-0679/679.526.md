# § 679.526 Filing-office rules.

The Department of State may adopt and publish rules to administer this chapter. The filing-office rules must be:

(1) Consistent with this chapter.

(2) Adopted and published in accordance with the Administrative Procedure Act.

History: s. 6, ch. 2001-198.
