# § 679.801 Saving clause.

(1) Except as otherwise provided in this part, this part applies to a transaction or lien within its scope, even if the transaction or lien was entered into or created before July 1, 2013.

(2) The amendments to this chapter by ch. 2012-59, Laws of Florida, do not affect an action, case, or proceeding commenced before July 1, 2013.

History: ss. 17, 19, ch. 2012-59.
