# § 252.81 Short title.

Part II of this chapter, consisting of ss. 252.81-252.90, may be cited as the “Florida Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act.”

History: s. 1, ch. 88-200; s. 1, ch. 92-150; s. 1, ch. 2005-5.
