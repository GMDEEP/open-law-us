# § 250.5395 Provision of training and facilities to support illegal drug reduction.

Subject to an annual appropriation, the Florida National Guard shall provide training, training support, and facilities to facilitate the state’s attainment of its goals to reduce the supply of and demand for illegal drugs.

History: s. 54, ch. 2003-68.
