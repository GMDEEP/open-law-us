# § 250.549 Authority of responding state required to relieve from assignment or reassign officers.

Nothing in the foregoing compact shall authorize or permit state officials or military officers of the requesting state to relieve from assignment or reassign officers or noncommissioned officers of National Guard units of the responding state without authorization by the appropriate authorities of the responding state.

History: s. 3, ch. 93-283.
