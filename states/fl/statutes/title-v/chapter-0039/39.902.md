# § 39.902 Definitions.

As used in this part, the term:

(1) “Domestic violence” has the meaning set forth in s. 741.28.

(2) “Domestic violence center” means an agency that provides services to victims of domestic violence as its primary mission.

(3) “Family or household member” has the meaning set forth in s. 741.28.
