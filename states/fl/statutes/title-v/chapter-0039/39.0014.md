# § 39.0014 Responsibilities of public agencies.

All state, county, and local agencies shall cooperate, assist, and provide information to the Office of Adoption and Child Protection and the department as will enable them to fulfill their responsibilities under this chapter.

History: s. 2, ch. 99-193; s. 2, ch. 2006-194; s. 3, ch. 2007-124.
