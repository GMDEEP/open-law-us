# § 26.031 Judicial circuits; number of judges.

The number of circuit judges in each circuit shall be as follows:

JUDICIAL CIRCUIT TOTAL

(1) First .......... 26

(2) Second .......... 17

(3) Third .......... 7

(4) Fourth .......... 37
