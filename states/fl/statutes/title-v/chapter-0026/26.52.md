# § 26.52 Travel expenses, circuit judges.

Each circuit judge shall be reimbursed for travel expenses as provided in s. 112.061.

History: s. 4, ch. 6912, 1915; RGS 3004; s. 2, ch. 8480, 1921; CGL 4738, s. 19, ch. 63-400.
