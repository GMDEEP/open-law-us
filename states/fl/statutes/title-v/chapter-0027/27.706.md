# § 27.706 Private practice of law prohibited.

Each capital collateral regional counsel and all full-time assistants appointed by him or her shall serve on a full-time basis and may not engage in the private practice of law.

History: s. 3, ch. 85-332; s. 150, ch. 95-147; s. 6, ch. 97-313.
