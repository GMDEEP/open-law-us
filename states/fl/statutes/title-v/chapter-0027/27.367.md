# § 27.367 State Attorneys Revenue Trust Fund.

The State Attorneys Revenue Trust Fund is created within the Justice Administrative Commission. Moneys credited to the trust fund shall be used for the purpose of funding the activities of the state attorneys.

History: s. 1, ch. 2009-8; s. 2, ch. 2011-16.
