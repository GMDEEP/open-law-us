# § 27.707 Investigators; service of process.

Each investigator employed by the capital collateral regional counsel has full authority to serve any subpoena or court order issued by any court or judge in any case for which the office has responsibility for providing representation.

History: s. 3, ch. 85-332; s. 7, ch. 97-313.
