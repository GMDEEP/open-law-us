# § 27.58 Administration of public defender services.

The public defender of each judicial circuit of the state shall be the chief administrator of all public defender services authorized under s. 27.51 within the circuit.

History: s. 7, ch. 63-409; s. 3, ch. 2000-343; s. 23, ch. 2003-402.
