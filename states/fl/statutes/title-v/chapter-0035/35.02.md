# § 35.02 First Appellate District.

The First Appellate District is composed of the First, Second, Third, Eighth, and Fourteenth Judicial Circuits.

History: s. 1, ch. 57-248; s. 1, ch. 65-294; s. 1, ch. 79-413; s. 5, ch. 2022-163.
