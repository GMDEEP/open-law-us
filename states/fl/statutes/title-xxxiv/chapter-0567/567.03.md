# § 567.03 Mode of holding election.

The election under s. 567.01 shall be held and conducted in the manner prescribed by law for holding general elections, except as herein provided.

History: s. 1, ch. 3700, 1887; RS 859; GS 1211; CGL 1936 Supp. 4151(198).
