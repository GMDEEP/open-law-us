# § 567.10 Refund of unused portion of municipal license tax.

Any municipality in such county voting by election to discontinue permitting the sale of intoxicating liquors, wines, or beer, shall refund to the licensee the fee for the unexpired and unused portion of any such license issued to him or her by said municipality.

History: s. 3, ch. 19140, 1939; s. 1, ch. 20719, 1941; s. 879, ch. 97-103.
