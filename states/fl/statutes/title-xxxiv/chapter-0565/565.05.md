# § 565.05 Purchase of distilled spirits by licensed clubs; size of individual containers.

It is unlawful for any person holding a license as a club for the sale of distilled spirits to purchase any of said distilled spirits in individual containers larger than 1.75 liters or 59.18 ounces, or smaller than 0.50 liter or 16.9 ounces, except for golf clubs licensed pursuant to s. 561.20(7)(b), which may purchase 50-milliliter or 1.7-ounce containers.
