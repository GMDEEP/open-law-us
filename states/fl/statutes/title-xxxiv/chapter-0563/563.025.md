# § 563.025 Surtax on license fees.

Each vendor of malt beverages containing alcohol of 0.5 percent or more by volume shall pay an annual surtax in an amount equal to 40 percent of the license fee imposed by s. 563.02(1).

History: s. 23, ch. 86-269.
