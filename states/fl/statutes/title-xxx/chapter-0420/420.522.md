# § 420.522 Legislative findings.

It is hereby found by the Legislature that:

(1) There continues to exist a serious shortage of affordable housing for farmworkers, very-low-income persons and families, and low-income persons and families throughout the state.

(2) Existing private, local, state, and federal resources have not been adequate to remedy this shortage.

(3) The provision of housing predevelopment funds is necessary to leverage currently available resources for housing development.

History: s. 13, ch. 88-376; s. 17, ch. 93-181.

Note. — Former s. 420.304.
