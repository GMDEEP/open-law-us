# § 413.4455 Deposit and appropriation of funds recovered under s. 413.445.

Funds received under the provisions of s. 413.445 shall be deposited in the Federal Rehabilitation Trust Fund and shall be available for appropriation for programs contained in this part.

History: s. 12, ch. 87-320.
