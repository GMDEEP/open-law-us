# § 413.202 Designated administrative unit.

Effective on the effective date of this act, for the purposes of effecting compliance with the Vocational Rehabilitation Act of 1973, as amended, the Division of Vocational Rehabilitation is designated as the administrative unit.

History: s. 8, ch. 2002-22.
