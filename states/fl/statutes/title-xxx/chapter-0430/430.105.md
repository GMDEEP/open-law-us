# § 430.105 Confidentiality of information.

Personal identifying information in a record held by the department that relates to an individual’s health or eligibility for or receipt of health-related, elder care, or long-term care services is confidential and exempt from s. 119.07(1) and s. 24(a), Art. I of the State Constitution. Such information may be disclosed:

(1) To another governmental entity for the purpose of administering the department’s programs for the elderly; or

(2) If the affected individual or his or her legal representative provides written consent.

History: s. 1, ch. 2001-194; s. 1, ch. 2006-112.
