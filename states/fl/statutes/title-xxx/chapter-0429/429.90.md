# § 429.90 Purpose.

The purpose of this part is to develop, establish, and enforce basic standards for adult day care centers in order to assure that a program of therapeutic social and health activities and services is provided to adults who have functional impairments, in a protective environment that provides as noninstitutional an atmosphere as possible.

History: s. 1, ch. 78-336; s. 2, ch. 81-318; ss. 79, 83, ch. 83-181; ss. 1, 17, ch. 93-215; s. 4, ch. 2006-197.

Note. — Former s. 400.55.
