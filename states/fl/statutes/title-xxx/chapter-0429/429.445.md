# § 429.445 Compliance with local zoning requirements.

No facility licensed under this part may commence any construction which will expand the size of the existing structure unless the licensee first submits to the agency proof that such construction will be in compliance with applicable local zoning requirements. Facilities with a licensed capacity of less than 15 persons shall comply with the provisions of chapter 419.

History: s. 2, ch. 85-251; s. 24, ch. 91-263; ss. 31, 39, ch. 93-216; s. 2, ch. 2006-197.
