# § 373.569 Bond election.

When required by the State Constitution, the governing board shall call an election of the freeholders in said district, in which said election the matter of whether or not said bonds shall be issued shall be decided as provided by law with respect to bond elections.

History: s. 37, ch. 25209, 1949; s. 25, ch. 73-190.

Note. — Former s. 378.37.
