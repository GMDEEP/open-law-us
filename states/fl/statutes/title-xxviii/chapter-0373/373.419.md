# § 373.419 Completion report.

Within 30 days after the completion of construction or alteration of any stormwater management system, dam, impoundment, reservoir, appurtenant work, or works, the permittee shall file a written statement of completion with the governing board or department. The governing board or department shall designate the form of such statement and such information as it shall require.

History: s. 6, part IV, ch. 72-299; s. 16, ch. 89-279.
