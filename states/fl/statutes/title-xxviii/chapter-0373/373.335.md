# § 373.335 Clearinghouse.

The department, in conjunction with the water management districts, shall establish a statewide clearinghouse which will allow each water management district to access information regarding water well contractor licensees and their license numbers, any violation by any such licensee, and any disciplinary action taken by a water management district.

History: ss. 18, 24, ch. 88-242; s. 4, ch. 91-429.
