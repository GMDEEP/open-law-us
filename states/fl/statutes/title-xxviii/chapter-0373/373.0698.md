# § 373.0698 Creation and operation of basin boards; other laws superseded.

The provisions of ss. 373.0693-373.0697 shall govern the creation and operation of basin boards within any water management district, the provisions of any other general or special law to the contrary notwithstanding.

History: s. 5, ch. 84-341.
