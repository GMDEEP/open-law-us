# § 373.113 Adoption of rules by the governing board.

In administering the provisions of this chapter the governing board has authority to adopt rules pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement provisions of law conferring powers or duties upon it.

History: s. 20, part I, ch. 72-299; s. 83, ch. 98-200.
