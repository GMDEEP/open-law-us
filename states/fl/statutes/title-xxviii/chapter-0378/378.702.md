# § 378.702 Existing mines.

On October 1, 1986, all operators of existing mines for fuller’s earth clay resource extraction shall meet the performance standards provided by s. 378.703 on all mines that increase the diameter of an existing mine.

History: s. 1, ch. 86-294.
