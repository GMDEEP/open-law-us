# § 378.802 Existing extraction locations.

After January 1, 1989, all operators of existing locations for the extraction of resources as described in s. 378.801 shall meet the performance standards provided by s. 378.803 for any new surface area disturbed at such locations.

History: s. 1, ch. 86-294; s. 1, ch. 89-88; s. 24, ch. 2021-188.
