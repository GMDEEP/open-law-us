# § 380.506 Support services.

The department shall furnish administrative, personnel, and other support services necessary for the trust to accomplish the purposes of this part.

History: s. 28, ch. 89-175; s. 2, ch. 90-192; s. 9, ch. 91-192; s. 5, ch. 91-429.
