# § 379.237 Courts of equity may enjoin.

Courts of equity in this state have jurisdiction to enforce the conservation laws of this state by injunction.

History: s. 2, ch. 61-231; s. 2, ch. 98-227; s. 95, ch. 99-245; s. 1, ch. 2002-264; s. 60, ch. 2008-247.

Note. — Former s. 370.021(10).
