# § 379.3711 License fee for private game preserves and farms.

The license fee for establishing, maintaining, and operating a private preserve and farm pursuant to s. 379.302 is $50 per year. The fee is payable to the commission and shall be deposited in the State Game Trust Fund.

History: s. 157, ch. 2008-247.
