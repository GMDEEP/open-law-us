# § 379.35 Review of fees for licenses and permits; review of exemptions.

The fees for licenses and permits established under this chapter, and exemptions thereto, shall be reviewed by the Legislature during its regular session every 5 years beginning in 2000.

History: s. 15, ch. 99-353; s. 130, ch. 2008-247.

Note. — Former s. 372.5711.
