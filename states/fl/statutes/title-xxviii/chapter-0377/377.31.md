# § 377.31 Evidence of rules and orders.

A certified copy of any division rule, regulation, or order shall be received in evidence in all courts of this state with the same effect as the original thereof.

History: s. 25, ch. 22819, 1945; ss. 25, 35, ch. 69-106; s. 23, ch. 78-95.
