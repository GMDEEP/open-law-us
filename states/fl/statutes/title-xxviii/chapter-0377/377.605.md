# § 377.605 Use of existing information.

The department may utilize to the fullest extent possible any existing energy information already prepared for state or federal agencies. Every state, county, and municipal agency shall cooperate with the department and shall submit any information on energy to the department upon request.

History: s. 5, ch. 74-186; s. 2, ch. 78-25; s. 8, ch. 91-113; s. 51, ch. 2008-227; s. 509, ch. 2011-142.
